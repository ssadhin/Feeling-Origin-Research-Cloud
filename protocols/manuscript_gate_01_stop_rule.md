# MANUSCRIPT-GATE-01 — stop rule and claim classification

Frozen: 2026-10-05, after CASE G1/G2 and POPANE-R1 level results, while O002-D1, POPANE-R2c, and POPANE-P1 outcomes are still unknown.

## Purpose
Prevent indefinite post-hoc robustness testing and define what evidence is sufficient to move from research exploration to manuscript drafting.

## Already established
1. CASE: real aligned peripheral physiology signal was previously demonstrated by the frozen shifted-physiology control.
2. CASE: recent-state self-report strongly predicts future valence/arousal under simultaneous unseen-person × unseen-video evaluation.
3. CASE: no positive physiology increment for absolute level forecasting at 1/11/21/31 s; valence often significantly worsens.
4. POPANE-R1: independent valence replication, 186 usable participants and 18 segments, Ridge + HGB, simultaneous held-person × held-segment; physiology is significantly worse than recent-state persistence at all 1/11/21/31 s horizons.
5. Bounded LIT-01 screen found no exact prior match, but important partial predecessors exist and database coverage is incomplete.

## Remaining mandatory gates
A. O002-D1 — CASE future-change target.
B. POPANE-R2 — independent POPANE future-change valence target. Technical reruns do not alter its frozen scientific protocol.
C. POPANE-P1 — signal-validity positive control for the external physiology feature pipeline.
D. Static protocol/code conformance audit for primary and external analyses.
E. Final literature/citation-chain audit with transparent database-access limitation.

## Claim classification after A–E

### CLASS A — MANUSCRIPT-READY NARROW BENCHMARK RESULT
Requirements:
- CASE-D1: no robust physiology increment for change according to frozen rule.
- POPANE-R2: no robust external physiology increment for valence change according to frozen rule.
- POPANE-P1: aligned physiology signal is valid or at least model-specific/credible, so external negative forecast is not reducible to an obviously dead feature pipeline.
- No material code/protocol violation.
- No exact prior paper found in the bounded final novelty screen.

Allowed core claim:
“In the tested continuous-affect settings, strong recent-state persistence dominates peripheral-physiology models for prospective level prediction, and physiology does not show robust incremental utility for short-horizon changes under strict person/stimulus generalization; the valence level result replicates externally.”

### CLASS B — MANUSCRIPT-READY NARROW LEVEL-FORECAST RESULT
Trigger:
- A robust physiology change signal appears in CASE or POPANE.

Required narrowing:
- Retain the absolute-level forecasting result.
- Explicitly report that physiology may contribute to predicting transitions/deviations.
- Do NOT say “no prospective utility” generically.
- If the positive dynamic effect is only in CASE and not external, label it dataset-specific/mixed.

### CLASS C — SERIOUS RESULT, MORE VALIDATION NEEDED
Trigger:
- POPANE-P1 shows no evidence that extracted physiology contains aligned signal, OR
- code audit identifies a material implementation ambiguity that cannot be resolved from frozen artifacts, OR
- novelty audit finds an essentially exact predecessor.

Action:
- Do not add same-dataset fishing analyses.
- Resolve only the identified validity dependency, then reassess.

### CLASS D — CLAIM FAILS
Trigger:
- A discovered code/leakage error invalidates the primary benchmark comparison, or
- corrected analysis reverses the key result under the originally intended contract.

Action:
- State failure and retire the claim rather than rescue it with a new endpoint.

## Stop rule
After mandatory gates A–E, do not add further CASE/POPANE analyses merely because results are inconvenient or reviewers might imagine another model. New analyses require either:
1. a concrete discovered implementation error;
2. an explicit reviewer/editor request;
3. a genuinely independent preregistered replication;
4. a clearly separate new research question.

## Non-negotiable firewalls
No result in this project supports:
- physiological causal irrelevance;
- body-independence of emotion or feeling;
- universal inability of physiology to forecast affect;
- self-report persistence as a causal mechanism;
- phenomenal-consciousness conclusions.
