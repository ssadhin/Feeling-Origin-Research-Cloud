# LIT-01 addendum — Mercado-Diaz et al. 2026 EDA-Graphs

Paper: Continuous Emotion Recognition Using EDA-Graphs: A Graph Signal Processing Approach for Affective Dimension Estimation
Applied Sciences 16(7):3240 (2026), DOI 10.3390/app16073240.

Classification: CLOSE CONTEMPORARY CASE/PHYSIOLOGY PREDECESSOR, NOT AN EXACT PROSPECTIVE-BENCHMARK MATCH.

Key overlap:
- Uses CASE continuous valence/arousal and peripheral physiology.
- Develops EDA-graph features and compares against traditional EDA and other physiological modalities.
- Uses Leave-One-Subject-Out cross-validation across all 30 CASE participants.
- Reports strong concurrent affect-dimension estimation and external classification generalization.

Key distinctions:
- Target is time-aligned/concurrent continuous emotion recognition, not non-overlapping future-window forecasting.
- Evaluation is unseen-person, but not simultaneous unseen-person × unseen-video in the primary CASE regression.
- Accessible methods do not use an observed recent self-report / parameter-free persistence baseline.
- It therefore answers whether physiology can recognize affect from aligned physiology, not whether physiology adds future predictive utility conditional on already knowing recent affect.

Interpretive consequence:
This paper is important positive evidence that sophisticated physiology-only features can predict CASE affect under LOSO, and it prevents any rhetoric that our negative conditional forecast implies physiology lacks affect information. It also makes the distinction between concurrent recognition and conditional prospective forecasting central to the introduction/discussion.

Novelty wording should explicitly acknowledge contemporary CASE physiology work rather than implying CASE physiology has not been predictive in prior studies.
