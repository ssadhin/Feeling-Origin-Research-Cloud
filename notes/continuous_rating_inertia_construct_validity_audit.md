# Continuous-rating inertia / construct-validity audit

Status: interpretive limitation identified during final paper-readiness review.

## Issue

Both CASE and POPANE use continuously/densely updated self-reported affect trajectories. A recent-state persistence baseline can be strong for at least two nonexclusive reasons:

1. genuine short-term persistence of subjective affect;
2. persistence introduced by the measurement process itself — motor/joystick/electronic-scale continuity, finite reaction time, reluctance to move the rating continuously, cognitive integration over recent experience, or other annotation smoothing.

The current forecasting analyses do not identify how much of persistence comes from latent affect dynamics versus the dynamics of reporting measured affect.

## Why this does not invalidate the benchmark result

The target actually evaluated by the models is the recorded continuous self-report trajectory. For that prediction target, a strong recent-observation baseline is a valid and practically important benchmark regardless of why the target is autocorrelated.

The central methodological result remains: models using peripheral physiology should demonstrate incremental predictive utility over strong state-history baselines when the outcome itself has substantial temporal persistence.

The 11/21/31 s horizon results and future-change attacks reduce—but do not eliminate—the possibility that the whole result is a trivial one-sample motor carryover artifact.

## Claim boundary

Permitted:
- “recent observed affect/persistence strongly predicts future recorded self-report”
- “peripheral physiology did not add robust short-horizon prospective utility for the measured continuous-affect trajectories under the tested designs”
- “benchmark choice materially changes conclusions about physiology-based affect forecasting”

Avoid:
- “the feeling itself simply persists”
- “persistence is the causal generator of subjective experience”
- “physiology is unrelated to latent emotional change”
- interpreting joystick/scale inertia as a property of phenomenal consciousness.

## Recommended manuscript limitation

State explicitly that the study predicts recorded continuous self-report, which combines affective dynamics with response/annotation dynamics. Future work should use designs that can separate reporting latency/smoothing from latent-state dynamics, such as independently timed probes, alternative response devices, latent-state measurement models, or datasets with validated annotation-lag estimates.

This limitation is not a reason to weaken the benchmarking contribution; it is a reason to keep the psychological interpretation narrower than the predictive result.
