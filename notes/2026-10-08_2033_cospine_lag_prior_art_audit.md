Prior-art direct anchors:
1. May et al. 2012, European Journal of Pain, DOI 10.1002/j.1532-2149.2011.00023.x: repeated fixed heat stimulation shows within-session sensitization and between-session habituation, a clear stimulus-history rival.
2. Anderson et al. 2013, European Journal of Pain, DOI 10.1002/j.1532-2149.2012.00190.x, PMID 22899549: among 312 adults fixed-heat temporal summation varies greatly, including unchanged and decreasing trajectories; group-average lag is not mechanistic.

4. Repetitive thermal stimulus adaptation and sensitization, PMC3114199 (2011): initial decline and later rise in pain ratings within fixed-stimulus series.
5. Site-specific habituation and site-nonspecific sensitization in thermal pain, PMID 24768695 (2014): opposing sequential effects across body sites.
6. Pain intensity vs unpleasantness emotion priming, Lyu et al. 2019 preprint arXiv:1902.05899: distinct late gamma associations, correlational, prior art not causal P.
