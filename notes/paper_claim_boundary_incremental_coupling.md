# Paper claim boundary — prediction utility vs physiology–experience coupling

## Two different scientific tasks must not be conflated

1. Sensor-only affect recognition: predict self-reported affect when self-report is unavailable. For that engineering task, a prior self-report baseline may be unavailable at deployment, and physiology can be useful even if it adds nothing after conditioning on recent report.

2. Incremental physiology–experience coupling: ask whether physiology contains prospective information about future reported feeling beyond what is already known from recent reported experience and context. This is the current paper track.

The O001/O002 results address task 2. They do not invalidate sensor-only emotion-recognition work and should not be advertised as showing that physiological sensing is useless.

## Current candidate scientific contribution

Use recent-report persistence as a strong comparator for short-horizon continuous affect forecasting, then measure the incremental value of EEG/autonomic/peripheral physiology under held-out-person evaluation, external replication, nonlinear sensitivity, temporal-alignment controls and lag-depth attacks.

## Current claim boundary

Allowed if robustness survives: physiology-only or physiology-rich models can carry affect-related signal, yet the incremental cross-person forecasting information supplied by physiology can be small, fragile or redundant after conditioning on recent reported state and context.

Forbidden: physiology does not matter for emotion; feelings are not embodied; bodily feedback is unnecessary; subjective feeling is generated by self-report history; the model identifies consciousness.

## Next generalization pressure after current P1/H1

Because EPiC 2025 emphasizes multiplicative generalizability, the next publication-level robustness candidate is a joint unseen-person × unseen-stimulus evaluation on CASE. Each test cell would hold out one subject and one video simultaneously, train on neither that subject nor that video, and compare time/context, prior-report, and physiology-augmented models. This should be frozen only after checking compute/identifiability and should not be used to redefine O002 primary.
