# CRFI Prior-Art Collision and Theory Status After V014

## Status

CRFI is **not currently established as a novel consciousness theory**.

The most defensible current description is:

> **a convergent hybrid candidate / engineering architecture whose components substantially overlap GNW, RPT/recurrency frameworks, IIT, and the July 2026 N* manuscript program.**

This does not show that CRFI is false. It shows that the project has not yet earned a distinct-theory claim, and it invalidates earlier internal language that treated CRFI as a clear winner over serious external rivals.

## Prior-art attack

### 1. Global Neuronal Workspace already contains bidirectional local↔workspace recurrence

GNW is not accurately represented by the old internal rival label “global gate only.”

A published GNW description states that local contents enter the workspace through ignition, are sustained by recurrent activity among GNW neurons and local processors, and that active workspace neurons send top-down amplification to active processor neurons while their bottom-up signals help sustain workspace activity.

Relevant sources:

- Mashour et al. (2020), *Conscious Processing and the Global Neuronal Workspace Hypothesis*, Neuron. PMID 32135090. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32135090/
- Zacks & Jablonka (2023), *The evolutionary origins of the Global Neuronal Workspace in vertebrates*, Neuroscience of Consciousness. https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niad020

**Consequence:** content admission + recurrent global maintenance + top-down return to content processors is prior art. Direct reciprocity cannot by itself serve as CRFI novelty.

### 2. Recurrent processing is already a major phenomenal-consciousness proposal

RPT treats recurrent/feedback processing in sensory systems as central and, in many formulations, sufficient for phenomenal consciousness without requiring prefrontal global access.

Relevant sources:

- Lamme & Roelfsema (2000), *The distinct modes of vision offered by feedforward and recurrent processing*. PMID 11074267.
- 2026 integrative review: *Consciousness in the Brain: An Integrative Review of Contemporary Theories*. PMID 42512519.
- Sep 2026 review: *Loops all the way up: Recurrency as an implementation-first primitive for consciousness*. PMID 42556031.

**Consequence:** recurrence itself is not a CRFI discovery. Any CRFI claim must distinguish its exact causal organization from strong local and global recurrent accounts.

### 3. IIT already ties qualitative character to intrinsic cause-effect structure

IIT explicitly proposes an identity between experience and intrinsic causal structure. IIT 4.0 states that the distinctions and relations in a substrate's cause-effect structure account for the quality of experience. Earlier IIT work describes experience as a structure/form in cause-effect space and argues that identical activity patterns in differently connected systems can correspond to different qualia.

Relevant sources:

- Albantakis et al. (2023), *Integrated information theory (IIT) 4.0*. PMID 37847724. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37847724/
- Balduzzi & Tononi (2009), *Qualia: the geometry of integrated information*. PMID 19680424.
- Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi (2014), IIT 3.0. PMID 24811198.

**Consequence:** “quality is causal-relational structure rather than activation pattern alone” is not uniquely CRFI.

### 4. N* is unusually close prior art

Phil Stilwell's July 2026 manuscript series predates this project's CRFI formulation.

The N* presence proposal states that, under viability, phenomenal presence requires the conjunction of:

- integration/synergy,
- counterfactual system-wide availability,
- recurrent stability.

Its phenomenal-character extension then proposes:

- content-specific **causal-trajectory geometry**,
- perturbation-derived neighborhoods,
- held-out phenomenal similarity and intervention tests,
- an independently anchored quality space,
- explicit recognition that relational evidence is invariant under automorphisms/inversions,
- additional anchors to break symmetry.

Relevant manuscript records:

- Stilwell (2026), *Cø as N*: A Minimal Network-Dynamics Model of Phenomenal Consciousness*. https://philarchive.org/rec/STICAN
- Stilwell (2026), *From Phenomenal Presence to Phenomenal Character: A Dynamical-Geometry Extension of the N* Model*. https://philarchive.org/rec/STIFPP-2
- Stilwell (2026), *Ablating N*: Does Every Conjunct Earn Its Place?*. https://philarchive.org/rec/STIANW

These are manuscripts, not peer-reviewed validation. They nevertheless constitute important prior art.

**Consequence:** the CRFI presence/quality split, perturbation-defined causal trajectory geometry, symmetry problem, held-out testing and anchor move cannot currently be claimed as independently novel.

## Current kill / reduction condition

> **If every operational CRFI commitment can be mapped onto an existing strong theory, or a straightforward conjunction of existing mechanisms, without producing a new divergent prediction, CRFI is not a distinct theory and must be reduced to a synthesis / implementation framework.**

This is now an explicit project-level kill condition.

## What would count as legitimate CRFI novelty now?

A candidate residual must satisfy all of the following:

1. It is stated **before** examining confirmatory data.
2. It makes a prediction that differs from strong GNW, IIT, RPT and N* under matched conditions.
3. The difference is not produced by a weaker implementation of the rival.
4. The prediction is not true merely because the CRFI simulator hard-codes the defining edge.
5. It can eventually be connected to an independent biological or phenomenological measurement.
6. Failure causes a reduction or rejection, not a post-hoc redefinition.

## Immediate implication for V015

The previously proposed V015 test—perturbing the recurrent domain and asking whether local content changes—is **not sufficient as a novelty test**, because GNW already contains top-down workspace-to-processor amplification and RPT already centers feedback.

V015 should be withheld until the theory decomposition yields a genuinely divergent prediction.

## Bottom line

The research has become more constrained, but also more honest:

- V014 falsified the first serious CRFI-specific behavioral signature.
- GNW removes reciprocity as a novelty claim.
- IIT removes causal-quality identity as a novelty claim.
- N* independently overlaps the combined presence/trajectory-geometry/anchor program with unusual specificity.

The next discovery, if there is one, has to survive **these theories as they actually are**, not simplified shadows of them.
