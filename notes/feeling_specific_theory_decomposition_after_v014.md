# Feeling-Specific Theory Decomposition After V014

## Status

**Hypothesis-generation / adversarial decomposition only. V015 remains withheld.**

V014 falsified the preregistered behavioral-uniqueness claim for CRFI. The subsequent prior-art attack showed that recurrence, global feedback, content admission, intrinsic causal structure, and perturbation-defined causal geometry are not individually novel CRFI commitments.

This note changes the target of the next attack from generic consciousness architecture to the more specific question that motivated the project:

> **Where is the physical state that bears a feeling?**

The aim is not to save CRFI. The aim is to determine whether a residual CRFI commitment exists that makes a genuinely different, prespecified prediction from strong affective theories. If not, CRFI must be reduced to a synthesis/engineering framework.

## 1. Affective rivals that must now be treated as first-class theories

### HOTEC — LeDoux & Brown higher-order theory of emotional consciousness

Primary source:
- LeDoux, J. E. & Brown, R. (2017), *A higher-order theory of emotional consciousness*, PNAS 114(10):E2016-E2025. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1619316114. PMID: 28202735. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5347624/

Strong reading:
- Conscious emotional feelings are higher-order states assembled in a cortical general network of cognition (GNC).
- First-order subcortical defensive/survival and body-sensing circuits provide lower-order, nonconscious inputs; they are not themselves directly responsible for conscious feeling.
- A higher-order representation can make lower-order information conscious.
- The theory explicitly predicts that damage to first-order subcortical defensive or body-sensing circuits should **mute but not eliminate** feelings of fear.
- The modified higher-order account also considers higher-order experience in the absence of a corresponding current first-order representation, as in hallucination/dream-like cases.

Adversarial importance:
HOTEC gives a direct alternative to any claim that the original lower-order evaluative source itself must be the feeling-bearing state.

### Damasio / Carvalho — interoceptive neural-nonneural hybrid

Primary sources:
- Carvalho, G. B. & Damasio, A. (2021), *Interoception and the origin of feelings: A new synthesis*, BioEssays 43:e2000261. DOI: 10.1002/bies.202000261. PMID: 33763881.
- Damasio, A. & Damasio, H. (2023), *Feelings Are the Source of Consciousness*, Neural Computation 35(3):277-286. DOI: 10.1162/neco_a_01521. PMID: 35896152. https://direct.mit.edu/neco/article/35/3/277/112379/Feelings-Are-the-Source-of-Consciousness

Strong reading:
- Feelings are conscious mental events representing body states under homeostatic regulation.
- Their proposed physiological basis is a distinctive two-way interaction between interoceptive neural systems and non-neural internal bodily components.
- The 2023 account explicitly rejects an isolated nervous system as sufficient and rejects inanimate objects as conscious.
- Homeostatic feelings are proposed as foundational/inaugural consciousness.

Adversarial importance:
Body-brain reciprocity is already serious prior art. Generic bidirectional causation therefore cannot distinguish CRFI. Damasio supplies a strong **biological-hybrid/substrate** rival.

### Seth / Friston — active interoceptive inference

Primary sources:
- Seth, A. K. (2013), *Interoceptive inference, emotion, and the embodied self*, Trends Cogn Sci. DOI: 10.1016/j.tics.2013.09.007. PMID: 24126130.
- Seth, A. K. & Friston, K. J. (2016), *Active interoceptive inference and the emotional brain*, Phil Trans R Soc B. PMID: 28080966. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5062097/

Strong reading:
- Descending predictions about bodily states engage autonomic reflexes.
- Ascending interoceptive signals update those predictions.
- The account explicitly describes **circular causality**, not a one-way readout.
- Emotional/affective content emerges from context-sensitive hierarchical interoceptive inference.
- Which part of this inferential hierarchy specifically supports conscious feeling is less settled than the causal architecture itself.

Adversarial importance:
Reciprocal body/brain prediction-error loops are also prior art. Any CRFI residual must be more specific than circular causality.

### Solms — affective consciousness / upper-brainstem homeostasis

Primary source:
- Solms, M. (2019), *The Hard Problem of Consciousness and the Free Energy Principle*, Front Psychol 9:2714. DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02714. PMID: 30761057. https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02714/full

Strong reading:
- Elemental consciousness is affect.
- Its proposed physiological mechanism is extended homeostasis centered in upper-brainstem systems.
- Loss of the relevant brainstem arousal machinery abolishes cortical consciousness, whereas loss of cortex is argued not to abolish basic affective consciousness.
- Cortex organizes/stabilizes cognition around affect rather than being the primary generator of affect.

Recent warning:
- Solms et al. (2026), *Inferring Affective Consciousness in an Artificial Agent: A Case Study*, arXiv:2609.03883, already explores affective-consciousness inference in a deterministic artificial agent. Artificial-agent affect research is therefore also occupied prior-art territory.

### N*, GNW, RPT, IIT remain required rivals

They remain necessary at the generic-consciousness level:
- N*: viability + integration/synergy + content-specific counterfactual system-wide availability + recurrent stability; substrate independent under constrained causal realization.
- GNW: ignition/global availability/recurrent workspace-local amplification.
- RPT: recurrent processing, often local sensory recurrence, as central to phenomenal consciousness.
- IIT: intrinsic cause-effect structure as constitutive of experience, with qualitative character tied to distinctions/relations in that structure.

N* sources:
- Phil Stilwell (2026), *Cø as N*: A Minimal Network-Dynamics Model of Phenomenal Consciousness*. https://philarchive.org/rec/STICAN
- Stilwell (2026), *Consciousness Without Report: What System-Wide Availability Actually Requires*. https://philarchive.org/rec/STICWR-2
- Stilwell (2026), *From Phenomenal Presence to Phenomenal Character: A Dynamical-Geometry Extension of the N* Model*. https://philarchive.org/rec/STIFPP-2

## 2. Candidate residual: CRFI-S (source-identity refinement)

This is **not yet accepted as CRFI doctrine**. It is a candidate residual to be attacked.

### Independent definition of the source

For a candidate feeling content f, define E_f without reference to consciousness as:

> the earliest/minimal causal state or subsystem whose intervention changes the current organism-relative evaluative/homeostatic variable that supplies the f-relevant content, before downstream global or higher-order re-representation.

The source label is frozen from causal origin, not assigned retrospectively to whichever node produces the desired result.

A downstream state P_f that encodes or re-represents E_f is a **proxy** unless intervention shows that it independently acquires the defining current-source relation.

### Candidate CRFI-S claim

> For a specific feeling content f, phenomenal attribution to that current evaluative content requires the independently identified source E_f itself to participate in a closed, content-specific reciprocal causal relation with an adequate domain S. A downstream proxy P_f that preserves decoded content, report, policy and global availability does not substitute for source participation merely by copying E_f.

This is stronger and narrower than generic recurrence, global broadcast, body-brain circularity, or causal integration.

### Why this is a legitimate candidate rather than automatically a rescue

It can only remain in the project if all of the following are accepted **before** confirmatory testing:

1. E_f is localized/defined independently of the CRFI classification.
2. P_f cannot be relabeled E_f after seeing results.
3. The relevant reciprocal edge/loop is not hard-coded as the measured outcome.
4. Rival implementations preserve their own constitutive machinery.
5. Failure of the source requirement removes CRFI-S rather than causing a new source definition.
6. The claim eventually connects to biological dissociations rather than living only inside a simulator.

If these conditions cannot be met, discard CRFI-S.

## 4. Strongest immediate lead: the source/proxy dissociation

The cleanest candidate is Intervention A because it directly pits two possible locations of feeling-bearing content against each other.

Minimal architecture:

Body/current viability -> E_f -> P_f -> S -> policy/report
                          ^           |
                          |-----------|

In the intact condition, S also returns causally to E_f.

In the critical source-decoupled condition:

Body/current viability -> E_f -> P_f <-> S -> policy/report

but **S -> E_f is selectively removed**, while:
- E_f still carries the same current evaluative variable,
- P_f retains an accurate representation of E_f,
- P_f/S recurrence, report and policy are held matched,
- source-to-proxy information remains available,
- ordinary behavior is matched as tightly as possible.

The scientific question is no longer whether one architecture behaves "more emotional." It is:

> **Do rival theories classify the same matched system differently depending on whether the original evaluative source or only its downstream proxy participates in the conscious-making causal organization?**

This is a theory-identification question, not a consciousness detector.

## 5. Why simulation alone cannot settle the feeling question

An artificial organism cannot provide ground-truth subjective feeling merely because a theory labels one condition conscious.

A V015 simulator would therefore have a narrower job:

1. verify that the proposed manipulations are causally selective;
2. verify that behavioral/report-level matching is genuinely achieved;
3. verify that strong implementations of the theories remain internally valid after the manipulation;
4. demonstrate that the frozen theories issue different classifications under those matched conditions;
5. derive intervention patterns that can later be connected to independent biological evidence.

A simulation may expose that the theories are operationally indistinguishable. That would itself be a useful negative result.

## 6. V015 admission gate

Do **not** run confirmatory V015 until all of these pass:

1. **Source criterion frozen** independently of CRFI outcome.
2. **HOTEC steelmanned** with its modified higher-order/absent-first-order cases.
3. **Damasio steelmanned** so neural-nonneural two-way interaction is not replaced by a generic "body signal."
4. **Seth/Friston steelmanned** with circular active interoceptive inference.
5. **Solms steelmanned** around upper-brainstem affect/extended homeostasis.
6. **N*/GNW/RPT/IIT** retained as consciousness-level controls.
7. At least one intervention yields genuinely divergent predictions without weakening a rival.
8. The divergence is not merely "CRFI contains edge X, therefore deleting X hurts CRFI."
9. A biological or phenomenological bridge measurement is specified in advance.
10. A failed CRFI-S prediction has an explicit reduction/rejection consequence.

## 7. Current kill condition

> **If source-specific reciprocal participation can be removed while preserving every independently operationalized CRFI-S consequence, or if strong HOTEC/Damasio/Seth/Solms/N*/GNW/RPT/IIT accounts can reproduce every CRFI-S classification without adding a distinct CRFI commitment, CRFI-S is rejected and CRFI remains a synthesis/engineering architecture rather than a distinct feeling theory.**

## 8. Current conclusion

The best lead after V014 is not another behavioral signature.

It is a **location/identity dissociation of the feeling-bearing state**:
- higher-order cortical construction (HOTEC),
- interoceptive neural-nonneural hybrid (Damasio),
- active interoceptive hierarchy/circular causality (Seth/Friston),
- upper-brainstem affective homeostasis (Solms),
- globally available/recurrent/integrated dynamics (GNW/RPT/N*/IIT),
- versus the provisional CRFI-S claim that the independently identified current evaluative source itself must be reciprocally embedded.

This is a sharper conflict than the V014 behavioral test, but it is not yet a CRFI victory or a V015 protocol.


## 9. Second prior-art collision: source-identity is not untouched territory

The source/proxy idea itself overlaps older consciousness theories and therefore **cannot presently be claimed as novel**.

### Self-representational / same-order theories

Relevant sources:
- Kriegel, U. (2007/2008), *The Same-Order Monitoring Theory of Consciousness*, Synthesis Philosophica 22(2):361-384. https://hrcak.srce.hr/23581
- Kriegel, U. & Williford, K. (eds.) (2006), *Self-Representational Approaches to Consciousness*, MIT Press.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, *Higher-Order Theories of Consciousness*, self-representational section.

These theories reject the idea that the conscious-making representation must be wholly independent of the state represented. In identity or part-whole versions, the conscious state itself carries or contains the relevant higher-order/self-representational relation.

This is not the same as CRFI-S: self-representation is an intentional/representational relation, whereas CRFI-S proposes a physical content-specific reciprocal causal relation between an independently identified evaluative source and a wider domain. But the broad idea that **the feeling/conscious state itself, rather than only a downstream proxy, must participate in its conscious-making relation** is clear prior art.

### Reflexive imperativism about affective phenomenal character

Relevant source:
- Barlassina, L. & Khan Hayward, M. (2019), *More of me! Less of me!: Reflexive Imperativism about Affective Phenomenal Character*, Mind 128(512):1013-1044. DOI: 10.1093/mind/fzz035.

Their proposal is specifically about affective phenomenal character: pleasant and unpleasant experiences are partly constituted by reflexive imperative content roughly equivalent to “more of me” / “less of me.”

Again, this is not a causal-network identity claim of the CRFI-S form. But it occupies affect-specific reflexive structure and further weakens any claim that reflexivity/source-self-involvement is a new discovery.

### Consequence

CRFI-S is now downgraded from “candidate novelty” to:

> **a provisional causal formalization that may be useful for adversarial theory identification, but whose novelty is unearned.**

To count as a distinct theory it would need a prediction that cannot be reproduced by:
1. higher-order construction;
2. self-/same-order representation;
3. reflexive affective imperativism;
4. biological interoceptive hybrid theories;
5. active interoceptive inference;
6. brainstem affective theories;
7. GNW/RPT/IIT/N*.

If it cannot, reduce it.

## 10. Preliminary biological bridge scan

The initial lesion literature gives constraints, not a decisive winner.

### Amygdala damage: route-specific fear survives

- Feinstein et al. (2013), *Fear and panic in humans with bilateral amygdala damage*, Nature Neuroscience 16:270-272. DOI: 10.1038/nn.3323. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3739474/

Three people with bilateral amygdala damage experienced strong CO2-evoked fear/panic, even though the best-studied patient had major deficits in fear to several external threat paradigms.

**Constraint:** “the source of fear” cannot be assigned wholesale to the amygdala. Source identification must be route- and content-specific. Interoceptive and exteroceptive routes can dissociate.

### Bilateral insula destruction: feelings can persist

- Damasio et al. (2013; published online 2012), *Persistence of Feelings and Sentience after Bilateral Damage of the Insula*, Cerebral Cortex 23(4):833-846. DOI: 10.1093/cercor/bhs077. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3657385/

The reported patient had complete bilateral insular destruction plus other damage, yet retained a broad range of body and emotional feelings. The authors argue against the insula as the exclusive source of feeling and propose subcortical plus cortical mapping levels.

**Constraint:** do not operationalize E_f as “insula activity” or any single cortical region by default.

### Brainstem lesions: strong necessity for arousal, weak specificity for feeling identity

- Parvizi & Damasio (2003), *Neuroanatomical correlates of brainstem coma*, Brain 126:1524-1536. DOI: 10.1093/brain/awg166. PMID: 12805123.
- Fischer et al. (2016), *A human brain network derived from coma-causing brainstem lesions*, Neurology 87:2427-2434.

Focal upper pontine/brainstem lesions can cause coma.

**Constraint:** this supports a causal role of brainstem systems in maintaining consciousness/arousal. It does **not by itself** establish that upper-brainstem affect is the constitutive feeling-bearing state, because loss of arousal globally removes the conditions needed to test felt-content localization.

### Spinal-cord deafferentation: potentially useful graded perturbation, not a clean knockout

Human spinal-cord injury partially disrupts afferent/efferent body-brain coupling. Studies report altered emotional/interoceptive processing, but the literature is not a clean abolition result and lesions spare multiple interoceptive routes.

**Constraint:** SCI may be useful for testing graded dependence of affect on body feedback, but is not presently a binary test of Damasio, HOTEC or CRFI-S.

## 11. Revised next move: Affective Causal Location Diagnostic Set

The most defensible next product is not “CRFI-S wins.” It is a **prespecified diagnostic set** that asks strong theories where the feeling-bearing state resides under matched information and behavior.

Candidate intervention families:
1. source-to-proxy preserved / return-to-source removed;
2. higher-order/GNC construction removed while lower interoceptive/affective loops remain;
3. living-body coupling replaced with a signal-matched artificial surrogate;
4. local self-/same-order relation disrupted while global availability is preserved;
5. counterfactual causal structure altered while current trajectories are replay-matched (not novel by itself; counterfactual-erasure prior art exists).

The goal is to find a *vector* of predictions that uniquely identifies or collapses theory families. A single manipulation need not distinguish every theory.

### Revised stop condition

If no small intervention set yields distinguishable, source-supported theory fingerprints without relying on unverifiable subjective labels, do not run V015. Record the theories as operationally underdetermined by the available paradigm.


## 12. Proxy-Equivalence Corollary: mechanism can be identifiable while phenomenal allocation is not

The source/proxy program exposes a project-specific form of interventional underdetermination.

Let:
- (M) be a fully specified physical system,
- (I) a prespecified family of feasible interventions,
- (O) the measured outcomes (behavior, report, physiology, neural dynamics, learning, etc.),
- (T_1) and (T_2) two theories that agree on the physical system and all intervention-outcome distributions but disagree on which internal state is the phenomenal bearer.

If, for every (i \in I),

[
P_{T_1}(O\mid do(i),M)=P_{T_2}(O\mid do(i),M),
]

then no experiment restricted to (I) and (O) can identify whether the phenomenal bearer is the source state (E_f), the proxy (P_f), both, or neither. The likelihood ratio contributed by those observations is 1.

Directly measuring that (S\to E_f) exists or has been cut can identify **causal architecture**. It does not by itself identify **phenomenal allocation** when the competing theories already agree about the measured architecture and differ only in their consciousness mapping.

This is not presented as a novel theorem. It is a direct identifiability consequence and is continuous with the project's earlier no-go result for causally inert qualia permutations.

### Falsifiability consequence

A source/proxy experiment can adjudicate theories only if at least one strong theory entails a difference in an independently assessable phenomenal/first-person structure or another observation that is not stipulated to be matched.

If CRFI-S says:
- report can stay the same,
- behavior can stay the same,
- all measured internal dynamics can stay the same except the defining source-return edge,
- but feeling disappears solely because that edge is absent,

then the experiment verifies the architectural manipulation but does **not** empirically verify the phenomenal claim.

Therefore V015 cannot use “different theory labels on the same matched agent” as confirmatory evidence for feeling identity.

## 13. Source-Relabeling Dilemma for CRFI-S

CRFI-S faces a second problem.

### Flexible source definition

If any downstream state (P_f) that accurately encodes/evaluates (f) may be reclassified as the new source (E_f), then the proposed source-return cut is not a genuine falsifier:

1. cut return to the original (E_f);
2. (P_f) remains recurrent/global and evaluative;
3. relabel (P_f) as (E_f);
4. declare CRFI-S satisfied again.

That move makes source identity outcome-dependent and violates the project's no-moving-goalposts rule.

### Rigid source definition

If (E_f) is frozen as the earliest/minimal causal origin of the current organism-relative evaluative variable, then downstream or centrally generated affective states can become genuine counterexamples. False interoceptive feedback, cognitive appraisal, imagery, hallucination-like higher-order states, and route-specific fear dissociations become relevant because felt content may change without the original peripheral or canonical source changing in the corresponding way.

### Required resolution

Before any V015 protocol, CRFI-S must state a source criterion that is:
1. independent of the consciousness verdict;
2. invariant under the critical interventions;
3. applicable to exteroceptive, interoceptive, imagined and centrally generated affect;
4. incapable of post-hoc migration from (E_f) to (P_f);
5. connected to a measurable phenomenal prediction.

If no such criterion can be stated, CRFI-S is rejected as a distinct testable theory.

## 14. Existing human source/proxy dissociations

The source/proxy idea is not empirically untouched.

### Real peripheral perturbation

Khalsa et al. (2009), *Bolus isoproterenol infusions provide a reliable method for assessing interoceptive awareness*, Int J Psychophysiol. DOI: 10.1016/j.ijpsycho.2008.08.010, PMID: 18854201.

Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled isoproterenol infusions produced dose-dependent changes in heart rate and heartbeat/breathing sensations. This is a useful controlled peripheral-interoceptive perturbation.

### Real arousal crossed with false feedback

Gray et al. (2007), *Modulation of Emotional Appraisal by False Physiological Feedback during fMRI*, PLoS ONE. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0000546, PMID: 17579718.

The design crossed isometric exercise/no-exercise with true, asynchronous and false cardiac feedback. False increased-heart-rate feedback increased emotional intensity ratings of neutral faces and altered insula/amygdala activity. In that study, exercise itself did not produce the same appraisal effect.

Therefore a simple “real body arousal × false body feedback” factorial is already prior art and cannot be our novelty claim.

### False feedback without corresponding physiological change

Thornton & Hagan (1976), *A failure to explain the effects of false heart-rate feedback on affect by induced changes in physiological response*, Br J Psychol. DOI: 10.1111/j.2044-8295.1976.tb01522.x, PMID: 974449.

The study reported false-feedback effects on affect without support for the proposal that the affective effect was caused by induced physiological changes.

Carroll & Evans (1981), *Effects of heart-rate biofeedback and false feedback on reactions to stressful stimulation*, Percept Mot Skills, PMID: 7312525.

Direction of false feedback did not alter actual heart rate, yet differential unpleasantness/loudness ratings appeared in the false-feedback group.

These older studies are relevant because they weaken any crude rule that affective change must always be mediated by a corresponding peripheral cardiac change.

### False feedback can also change the body

Parrotta et al. (2026), *Exposure to false cardiac feedback alters pain perception and anticipatory cardiac frequency*, eLife. DOI: 10.7554/eLife.90013.

False faster cardiac feedback changed pain expectations and ratings and also altered anticipatory cardiac physiology. The control experiment strongly reduced these effects when feedback was framed exteroceptively.

This means that “proxy” manipulations can causally alter the putative source. A valid source/proxy experiment must therefore model/measure mediation and cannot assume the manipulation remains downstream.

### Contemporary review

Stevenson (2026), *Psychological induction of interoceptive states*, Consciousness and Cognition, PMID: 41687306, reviews psychologically induced interoceptive states, including cases without the normal physiological perturbation and cases where psychological induction modifies the body itself.

## 16. Better empirical target: mediation, not mere mismatch

Because proxy manipulations can either:
- change reported affect without a corresponding measured cardiac change, **or**
- feed back and alter actual physiology,

the sharper empirical quantity is a causal decomposition.

For a proxy manipulation (Z), source state (E), and first-person affect measure (Y):

- total proxy effect: (TE = Y(do(Z_1)) - Y(do(Z_0)));
- source-mediated component: effect transmitted through measured changes in (E);
- residual/direct component: proxy effect on (Y) not explained by the measured source pathway, subject to the usual causal-identification assumptions.

The research question becomes:

> When an interoceptive proxy changes felt affect, how much of that change is mediated by the current bodily/evaluative source, and how much remains when the source trajectory is matched?

This is scientifically sharper than merely asking whether false feedback “works.”

However, even a residual direct effect does not by itself locate phenomenality. It constrains theories only to the extent that they make prespecified claims about whether such a source-independent phenomenal effect is possible.

## 17. Revised V015 admission rule

V015 remains **WITHHELD**.

Before any confirmatory compute:
1. resolve or reject the Source-Relabeling Dilemma;
2. require at least one theory pair to make disjoint observable predictions, not merely different phenomenal labels;
3. specify an independently measured phenomenal/first-person outcome that is not forced to match by design;
4. distinguish mechanism identification from phenomenal-bearer attribution;
5. incorporate mediation because proxy manipulations can alter the source;
6. use existing false-feedback and peripheral-perturbation literature as prior constraints;
7. freeze failure consequences before simulation.

If these cannot be met, the correct result is **operational underdetermination**, not a new simulator.
