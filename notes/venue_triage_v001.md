# Venue triage v001 — 2026-10-05

Status: preliminary; final venue selection waits for MANUSCRIPT-GATE-01 classification.

## 1. Royal Society Open Science — strongest current fit
- Natural intellectual continuity with Coles et al. 2025 EPiC (same journal), which explicitly identified the need for stronger continuity/autocorrelation-preserving baselines.
- Broad interdisciplinary scope can accommodate affective science + physiology + predictive benchmarking without requiring a novel ML architecture.
- The present contribution is methodological/generalization-focused and replication-heavy, which is a good fit for a broad open-science journal.
- Risk: manuscript must make the contribution broader than a single benchmark tweak; external POPANE replication and strict double holdout are therefore central.

## 2. Affective Science — strong conceptual/methodological fit
- Publishes empirical and methods work spanning subjective affect, physiology, temporal dynamics and naturalistic measurement.
- Best if discussion foregrounds what benchmark choice changes about inference in affective science, rather than positioning the paper primarily as an ML benchmark.
- Risk: a highly technical cross-validation/ML presentation may need translation into affect-science consequences.

## 3. IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing — technically relevant but secondary
- Explicitly covers affect sensing, analysis, system evaluation, and physiological emotion recognition.
- Best if the paper is framed as an evaluation/benchmarking contribution for affective computing systems.
- Risk: no new architecture; reviewers may expect stronger algorithmic novelty. The methodological result can still matter, but RSOS/Affective Science currently look more natural.

## Provisional submission order
1. Royal Society Open Science

If the final paper becomes a narrower absolute-level forecasting result because a robust change signal survives, IEEE TAC may become relatively more attractive; if the main message remains benchmark choice and inferential discipline, RSOS is preferred.
