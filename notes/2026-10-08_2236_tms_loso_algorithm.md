Reproducibility algorithm: Parse first three CSV fields; group by subject. Keep subjects with >=8 sites. Center each person's SUDS by their own site mean. For each held-out person i, site s: offset(s) = mean centered SUDS for site s in OTHER eligible persons in same group. Estimate intercept_i(-s) = mean over other sites k of [SUDS(i,k)-offset(k)]. Predict SUDS(i,s)=intercept_i(-s)+offset(s). Baseline=mean SUDS(i,k) over k != s. Repeat with pooled-group offsets. MAE/RMSE on all held-out rows. 1000 within-person site-label permutations and 2000 person bootstraps; seed 20261008.
