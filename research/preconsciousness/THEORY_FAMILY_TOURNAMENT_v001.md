THEORY-FAMILY TOURNAMENT v001
Date: 2026-10-07
Program: Pre-Consciousness Layer Program
Question: Across the major scientific theory families, do self-maintenance, instinct, emotion, and feeling form four natural kinds in a strict depth sequence?
SCOPE RULE
This is a bounded-exhaustive theory-family tournament, not a claim to have enumerated every paper ever written. The search space is anchored to recent integrative reviews that identify the main emotion-theory families and to broad frameworks (Human Affectome, RDoC, Emotion Ontology) explicitly designed to integrate or standardize affective constructs.
TARGET CLAIMS
T1: Self-maintenance, instinct, emotion, and feeling are scientifically distinct.
T2: Each is a single natural kind.
T3: They form the universal strict order M -> I -> E -> F.
T4: They are exhaustive categories of human experience.
T5: Each can be given a scientific/mathematical operationalization.
TOURNAMENT
1. HOMEOSTASIS / ALLOSTASIS / COMPUTATIONAL BODY REGULATION
Representative sources:
- Ramsay & Woods 2014, DOI 10.1037/a0035942.
- Sterling 2012, DOI 10.1016/j.physbeh.2011.06.004.
- Petzschner et al. 2021, DOI 10.1016/j.tins.2020.09.012.
- Keramati & Gutkin 2014, DOI 10.7554/eLife.04811.
Result:
Strong support for a distinct self-maintenance/body-regulation construct. Formal control/RL/active-inference models exist. These models already distinguish internal physiological state, interoception, action selection, forecasting, and learned/habitual policies. They do not imply a single M -> instinct -> emotion -> feeling chain.
2. DEVELOPMENTAL SYSTEMS / ETHOLOGY OF INNATENESS
Representative sources:
- Mameli & Bateson 2011, DOI 10.1098/rstb.2010.0174.
- Blumberg 2017, DOI 10.1002/wcs.1371.
- Jiang & Pan 2022, PMID 35633465.
Result:
The ordinary word "instinct" fails as one natural kind. Inborn, genetically determined, independent of experience, species-typical, stereotyped, canalized, and unlearned are not equivalent. Innate behavior selection is modulated by internal state and environment. A scientifically usable construct must therefore be narrower: a developmentally canalized action-control disposition/policy bias.
3. BASIC-EMOTION / PANKSEPPIAN AFFECTIVE NEUROSCIENCE
Representative source:
- Selected Principles of Pankseppian Affective Neuroscience, 2019, PMCID PMC6344464.
Result:
Supports evolved emotional action systems, but explicitly distinguishes homeostatic affects such as HUNGER/THIRST from primary emotional affects. This alone blocks a universal rule that all feeling is downstream of emotion. Panksepp's account also treats affective feeling as central, so it does not map cleanly onto an emotion-state-without-feeling definition.
4. APPRAISAL / COMPONENT-PROCESS THEORY
Representative sources:
- Scherer 2005.
- Mulligan & Scherer 2012, DOI 10.1177/1754073912445818.
- Scherer & Moors 2019, DOI 10.1146/annurev-psych-122216-011854.
Result:
Strong support for emotion as a dynamic multi-component episode. The Scherer-Moors process explicitly places appraisal/elicitation and differentiated action/physiology before conscious representation/experience (feeling), then categorization/labeling. This supports E -> emotional-feeling within this theory family, but it does not establish instinct as a necessary precursor or broad feeling as exclusively emotional.
5. PSYCHOLOGICAL CONSTRUCTION / PREDICTIVE-ALLOSTATIC THEORY
Representative source:
- Barrett 2017, DOI 10.1093/scan/nsw154.
Result:
Challenges discrete emotion natural kinds and models emotional episodes as constructed using domain-general predictive/allostatic processes, interoception, concepts, and context. This directly attacks any universal claim that emotion must be a separate fixed layer generated by an instinct layer.
6. FUNCTIONAL-STATE / CROSS-SPECIES EMOTION
Representative sources:
- Anderson & Adolphs 2014, DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2014.03.003.
- Adolphs & Andler 2018, DOI 10.1177/1754073918765662.
Result:
Strongest support for our operational separation of emotion-state from feeling. Emotion can be treated as a central functional state with persistence, scalability, valence, generalization, and causal relations to behavior/cognition while conscious experience is bracketed. Supports E != F under an explicit functionalist definition.
7. SURVIVAL-CIRCUIT / HIGHER-ORDER EMOTIONAL CONSCIOUSNESS
Representative sources:
- LeDoux 2012, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2012.02.004.
- LeDoux & Brown 2017, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1619316114.
Result:
Separates nonconscious survival circuits/functions from conscious emotional experience. However, terminology differs: LeDoux often reserves emotion/fear language more narrowly for conscious experience. This is a terminology conflict, not evidence that nonconscious defensive control and conscious feeling are identical.
8. DAMASIO / BODY-STATE FEELING
Representative source:
- Damasio & Carvalho 2013, DOI 10.1038/nrn3403.
Result:
Provides a particularly clean conceptual separation:
homeostasis regulates life;
drives and emotions are action programs;
feelings are mental experiences of body states.
Crucially, feelings include hunger, pain, wellbeing, fear, anger, compassion, gratitude and love. Therefore drive-generated/homeostatic feelings supply a route to feeling that does not require a preceding full emotional episode.
9. HUMAN AFFECTOME
Representative source:
- Schiller et al., The Human Affectome, Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 2024-era final publication, PMCID PMC11003721.
Result:
Major prior-art pressure. This large integrative project organizes affect around nested/intertwined purposes:
ensure viability -> execute operations -> enact relevance -> entertain abstraction.
It explicitly includes sensation, emotion, mood, wellbeing, feeling, valence, arousal, motivation, stress, self, etc. It defines feeling as first-person conscious qualitative experience and subjective report as a measurement requiring higher-order metacognition, not as feeling itself.
This framework is strikingly compatible with the project's intuition that viability is deepest, but it rejects the idea that our four words are exhaustive or a simple serial ladder.
10. NIMH RDoC
Representative source:
- NIMH Definitions of RDoC Domains and Constructs.
Result:
Six major domains currently organize human functioning: negative valence, positive valence, cognitive systems, systems for social processes, arousal/regulatory systems, and sensorimotor systems. Arousal is explicitly distinguished from motivation and valence; sensorimotor action selection is separately defined; cognition and reward/motivation have distinct constructs.
This strongly rejects T4, the claim that M/I/E/F are an exhaustive taxonomy of human functioning or experience.
11. FORMAL EMOTION ONTOLOGY
Representative sources:
- Hastings, Ceusters, Smith & Mulligan 2011, Dispositions and Processes in the Emotion Ontology.
- Hastings et al. 2014, DOI 10.1186/2041-1480-5-38.
Result:
Formal prior art already distinguishes processes, representations, dispositions, action tendencies, emotional experiences, moods, etc. This supports the methodological move of separating ontological types. It also means a paper cannot claim novelty merely for saying that emotion terminology mixes dispositions, processes, and experiences.
12. 2024 COMPONENTIAL CONVERGENCE / "AFFECTIVISM"
Representative source:
- Pool & Sander / Affective Science 2024 "Affectivism and the Emotional Elephant".
Result:
Recent synthesis argues that five major theory families emphasize different components: appraisal theories emphasize cognition/elicitation, basic-emotion theory expression, constructionist theory feeling, motivational theories action tendency, and bodily/interoceptive theories autonomic activity. This provides a principled bounded theory-family coverage criterion. It supports componential pluralism rather than a single linear four-stage natural-kind ladder.
ADVERSARIAL VERDICT BY CLAIM
T1 — THE FOUR TARGETS ARE DISTINCT:
PARTIAL PASS.
They can be scientifically distinguished if:
M = viability regulation process;
I = narrowly defined canalized action-control disposition;
E = functional emotion-state episode;
F = conscious qualitative feeling.
The distinction is operational/type-based and produces different measurement/intervention predictions.
T2 — EACH IS ONE NATURAL KIND:
FAIL.
"Instinct" most clearly fails; broad feeling also decomposes into homeostatic/interoceptive, emotional, sensory and possibly other feeling families. Emotion's natural-kind status remains theory-contested.
T3 — UNIVERSAL STRICT M -> I -> E -> F ORDER:
FAIL.
Homeostatic/interoceptive feeling gives a route to F not requiring full E.
Emotion can be generated through learned/contextual/appraisal processes rather than requiring one narrow instinctive policy as proximal precursor.
Mature human systems are recurrent.
T4 — EXHAUSTIVE CATEGORIES OF HUMAN EXPERIENCE:
FAIL.
RDoC, Human Affectome, and ordinary affective-science distinctions require additional constructs including sensation/perception, mood, arousal, motivation, cognition, memory, social processing and volition. Moreover M and I are primarily mechanisms/dispositions rather than experiences.
T5 — SCIENTIFIC/MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION:
PASS FOR M; PROVISIONAL PASS FOR NARROW I AND FUNCTIONAL E; PARTIAL PASS FOR F.
M has established control/RL/active-inference formalizations.
I can be represented as a developmentally constrained prior/disposition over policies, but no field-standard scalar "instinct equation" exists.
E can be modeled as a latent dynamical state with multi-system outputs.
F can be given an observation/measurement model, but phenomenal feeling itself is not identified from report alone without a bridge theory of consciousness.
SURVIVING ANSWER
The four words do not form four exhaustive natural kinds or a universal ladder.
They can instead be made scientifically coherent by treating them as DIFFERENT ONTOLOGICAL/COMPUTATIONAL TYPES:
M = regulatory process/viability constraint.
I = canalized action disposition/policy prior.
E = occurrent latent dynamical coordination state.
F = first-person phenomenal/experiential state, observed indirectly through reports/behavior.
This typed separation is the leading project result.
