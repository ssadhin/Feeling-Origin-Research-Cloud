FOUR-CATEGORY ADJUDICATION v001
Date: 2026-10-07
Program: Pre-Consciousness Layer Program
Question: Are self-maintenance, instinct, emotion, and feeling four distinct and sufficient scientific categories, and do they form the order self-maintenance -> instinct -> emotion -> feeling?
FINAL VERDICT OF THIS PASS
A. DISTINCTNESS
The four labels point to genuinely different kinds of phenomena, but only after strong operational qualification:
1. self-maintenance = viability regulation;
2. "instinct" = not a clean natural kind and should be decomposed into canalized/innate action-control plus internal motivational/drive state;
3. emotion = a central functional state coordinating multiple response systems;
4. feeling = conscious/subjective experience, but itself splits into homeostatic/interoceptive feeling and emotional feeling.
B. EXHAUSTIVENESS
The claim that these are exactly the four categories of human experience is rejected.
Reasons:
- self-maintenance is mostly a biological regulatory process, not itself an experience;
- instinct is primarily a disposition/action-control architecture, not itself a subjective experience;
- motivation/drive is scientifically distinguishable from both innate action programs and emotion;
- sensation, perception, mood, arousal, motivation, cognition, memory, and volition are not exhausted by these four labels;
- broad feeling includes homeostatic feelings such as hunger, thirst, and pain that do not require a full emotional episode.
C. ORDER
The universal strict serial chain M -> I -> E -> F is rejected as a complete architecture.
The strongest surviving architecture is branching and recurrent.
D. SCIENTIFIC USEFULNESS
The original four labels remain useful as a research scaffold if they are not called exhaustive categories and if "instinct" is not treated as a single primitive scientific variable.
==================================================
1. SELF-MAINTENANCE
==================================================
Scientific status:
Strongly defensible as a distinct regulatory class.
Operational definition:
Endogenous physiological/cellular regulation that preserves or restores the organism within a viability-compatible region despite perturbation.
Formal candidate:
M_H(x_t) = P[x(t:t+H) remains within V | current state, regulation]
Supporting evidence:
- Ramsay & Woods (2014), DOI 10.1037/a0035942: homeostasis concerns maintenance/defense of vital physiological variables and distinguishes multiple regulatory loops.
- Bacterial homeostasis reviews show physicochemical regulation in single cells, establishing that self-maintenance does not require a nervous system, emotion, or feeling.
Boundary result:
Self-maintenance is deeper than nervous-system action control in the evolutionary/mechanistic sense because cellular homeostasis exists in organisms without nervous systems.
Conclusion:
M is retained.
==================================================
2. INSTINCT: REJECT AS SINGLE PRIMITIVE, DECOMPOSE
==================================================
Problem:
"Instinct" bundles properties that do not necessarily co-occur:
- species-typicality;
- developmental reliability;
- genetic influence;
- lack of task-specific learning;
- stereotypy;
- cue-triggering;
- motivational gating;
- fixed or modal action patterns.
Key sources:
- Mameli & Bateson (2011), DOI 10.1098/rstb.2010.0174: innateness is not a single scientific natural kind.
- Blumberg (2017), DOI 10.1002/wcs.1371: species-typical behaviors develop through species-typical developmental experience; "hardwired" and "genetically determined" are inadequate general definitions.
- Ronacher (2019), DOI 10.1007/s00359-018-01311-3: fixed action patterns and innate releasing mechanisms are useful in specific neuroethological systems, but modern work reveals mechanistic nuance.
- Reviews of innate action selection explicitly distinguish innate behaviors from internal states such as motivation, arousal, drive, and emotion.
Required split:
2A. CANALIZED / INNATE ACTION-CONTROL A(t)
A policy architecture that produces developmentally reliable species-typical action biases without requiring the same task-specific individual learning needed by acquired habits.
Candidate formal object:
P(pi_t | s_t, d, h)
where d = developmental conditions and h = individual learning history.
2B. MOTIVATIONAL / DRIVE STATE D(t)
An internal state that changes action probability, vigor, persistence, or incentive value as a function of organismic need and opportunity.
Evidence for separability:
- motivation and emotion overlap but are distinguishable in major reviews;
- incentive-salience "wanting" can dissociate from hedonic "liking";
- innate behavior selection is regulated by drive/motivation/emotion rather than being identical to them.
Conclusion:
The word "instinct" may remain an informal heading, but it is not acceptable as one mathematical state variable.
For scientific work, replace I with at least A + D.
==================================================
3. EMOTION-STATE
==================================================
Scientific status:
No universal definition, but a defensible functional construct exists.
Operational definition:
A temporally extended internal state that coordinates multiple changes in physiology, action readiness, attention/valuation, learning/decision processes, and expression in response to organism-relevant challenges or opportunities.
Key sources:
- Adolphs (2017), DOI 10.1093/scan/nsw153: distinguishes emotion states, emotion concepts, and conscious emotion experiences.
- Adolphs & Andler (2018), DOI 10.1177/1754073918765662: functional emotion states can be investigated separately from feelings.
- Anderson & Adolphs (2014), PMID 24679535: emotion states across phylogeny have features such as persistence, scalability, generalization, and valence.
- Scherer & Moors (2019), DOI 10.1146/annurev-psych-122216-011854: appraisal leads to action tendency/physiological differentiation before conscious representation/feeling in their component-process account.
Formal candidate:
e(t+1) = f_E(e(t), relevance_t, body_t, context_t)
y_E(t) = g_E(e(t)) + noise
Distinctness from action-control:
Action-control specifies which policy/motor program is selected.
Emotion-state is a persistent coordinating internal state that can alter many policies, perceptions, physiological variables, and learning processes.
Conclusion:
E is retained as a distinct research construct, but not as universally defined by the field.
==================================================
4. FEELING
==================================================
Scientific status:
Not unitary.
Key distinction:
F_homeo = homeostatic/interoceptive feeling
F_emotion = conscious emotional experience
F_report = explicit report/access
F_phen = phenomenal experience itself
Key sources:
- Damasio & Carvalho (2013), DOI 10.1038/nrn3403: feelings are mental experiences of body states; examples include hunger, pain, well-being, fear, anger, compassion, gratitude, love. Drives and emotions are action programs that can elicit feelings.
- Tsuchiya & Adolphs (2007), DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2007.01.005: emotion state and conscious feeling should be separated.
- LeDoux & Brown (2017), PMID 28202735: emotional consciousness is not identical to subcortical survival circuitry.
- Interoception literature emphasizes that many regulatory processes are unconscious while hunger, thirst, pain and related states can be consciously felt.
Critical result:
A broad "feeling" category cannot be placed only downstream of emotion, because homeostatic/interoceptive feelings may arise from internal body-state representation without a full emotion episode.
Conclusion:
F is retained only as a family with at least two primary subtypes: homeostatic feeling and emotional feeling.
==================================================
5. ADJACENT-LAYER SEPARATION RESULTS
==================================================
M vs A (self-maintenance vs canalized action-control)
Verdict: DISTINCT.
Evidence:
- cells/bacteria regulate pH, ions, energy state, macromolecular conditions without nervous-system action programs;
- spinal/central pattern generator literature shows organized motor pattern generation is a separable neural control problem.
Interpretation:
Self-maintenance is regulation of viability variables.
Action-control is selection/generation of behavior.
D vs A (drive/motivation vs action-control)
Verdict: DISTINCT BUT COUPLED.
Evidence:
- internal motivation/drive modulates selection and vigor of innate behaviors;
- the same action architecture can be expressed differently under different internal drives.
E vs A (emotion vs action-control)
Verdict: DISTINCT BUT COUPLED.
Evidence:
- emotion states persist, generalize, scale, and coordinate multiple systems;
- individual innate action patterns can be generated by specialized circuits/CPGs without thereby establishing a whole-organism emotion state.
E vs F_emotion
Verdict: DISTINCT UNDER FUNCTIONALIST DEFINITIONS; terminology remains theory-dependent.
Evidence:
- affective neuroscience distinguishes emotion-state from conscious experience;
- conditioned defensive/autonomic responses and threat processing can occur without conscious awareness in some paradigms;
- conscious fear is therefore not identical to every defensive response.
M/interoception vs F_homeo
Verdict: DIRECT BRANCH IS PLAUSIBLE AND IMPORTANT.
Evidence:
- hunger, thirst, pain, dyspnea, temperature discomfort, etc. are described as conscious homeostatic/interoceptive feelings;
- interoceptive processing is mostly unconscious, but a subset becomes consciously experienced.

6. ARCHITECTURE TOURNAMENT
==================================================
MODEL S: STRICT SERIAL
M -> I -> E -> F
Verdict: REJECT AS UNIVERSAL MODEL.
Failure 1:
"Instinct" is not one scientific primitive.
Failure 2:
Motivational/drive states cannot be safely collapsed into innate action patterns.
Failure 3:
Broad feeling includes homeostatic feelings that need not be downstream of a full emotion episode.
Failure 4:
Emotion can be elicited or shaped through learned, remembered, cognitive, and contextual routes; a specific innate policy is not a necessary proximal precursor to every emotion episode.
MODEL B: BRANCHING
M -> D
M -> interoceptive signals
D + sensory/context/history -> A
D + sensory/context/appraisal/history -> E
interoceptive signals + conscious-capable architecture -> F_homeo
E + conscious-capable architecture -> F_emotion
E -> A
A -> body/environment feedback -> M/D/E
Verdict: STRONGEST CURRENT MINIMAL MODEL.
MODEL R: RECURRENT MATURE HUMAN ARCHITECTURE
M <-> D <-> A <-> E
M/interoception -> F_homeo
E <-> F_emotion
F/cognition/volition -> A/E and indirectly M
Verdict:
Most plausible for mature humans, because cognition, voluntary regulation, feedback, learning, and interoception create recurrent loops.

7. THE EXACT "FOUR CATEGORIES" QUESTION
==================================================
Claim 1:
"These are four different things."
Adjudication:
QUALIFIED YES.
Self-maintenance, innate/canalized action-control, emotion-state, and conscious feeling are scientifically distinguishable kinds of process/state.
Claim 2:
"These are exactly the four categories humans experience."
Adjudication:
NO.
Self-maintenance and instinct/action-control are mostly mechanisms rather than subjective experiences, and the set omits other distinct process families such as motivation/drive, sensation/perception, mood/arousal, memory, cognition, and volition.
Claim 3:
"They occur in the strict order self-maintenance -> instinct -> emotion -> feeling."
Adjudication:
NO AS A UNIVERSAL LAW.
A branching/recurrent architecture fits the literature better.
Claim 4:
"Can the original four remain as our research scaffold?"
Adjudication:
YES, with explicit corrections:
- Self-maintenance stays.
- "Instinct" must be renamed or decomposed.
- Emotion must be defined independently of feeling.
- Feeling must split into homeostatic and emotional feeling.
- The arrows must be hypotheses, not definitions.

8. REVISED MINIMUM SCIENTIFIC ONTOLOGY
==================================================
For mechanistic work before consciousness, the minimum clean set is currently:
M — viability/self-maintenance regulation
D — motivational/drive internal state
A — action-control policy architecture (innate/canalized and learned subtypes)
E — emotion-state
F — conscious feeling family (homeostatic and emotional)
Reasoning/volition/cognition are later interacting systems, not simply a surface scalar.
This is a FIVE-CONSTRUCT minimum if the goal is mechanistic separation.
It may be compressed into four coarse domains only by combining D + A into one "preconscious behavioral-control" domain, but that compression loses a well-supported dissociation between wanting/motivation and action policy.

10. FINAL RESEARCH POSITION
==================================================
The research has made a decisive correction.
We should NOT claim:
"Science shows four exact human-experience categories in the order self-maintenance -> instinct -> emotion -> feeling."
We CAN claim:
"The project has identified a defensible set of separable regulatory, action-control, affective, and experiential constructs, but the evidence favors a branching/recurrent architecture and requires splitting the old instinct layer into motivation/drive and action-control, and splitting feeling into homeostatic and emotional forms."
This is the stable conclusion of the current literature-based adjudication unless new causal evidence overturns one of the separations.
