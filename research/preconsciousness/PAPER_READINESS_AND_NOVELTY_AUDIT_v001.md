Can the project produce a scientifically defensible paper about self-maintenance, instinct, emotion, and feeling, after the strict four-step ladder has failed?
SHORT ANSWER
Yes, but not as a paper claiming discovery of four exhaustive natural kinds or proof of the sequence self-maintenance -> instinct -> emotion -> feeling.
The strongest potentially publishable contribution is a formal construct-separation and causal-identifiability framework showing that four commonly conflated terms occupy different ontological/computational roles, while the usual verbal ladder is neither exhaustive nor causally identified.

1. CLAIMS THAT MUST NOT BE MADE
==================================================
KILL 1 — "We discovered the four fundamental categories of human experience."
Rejected. Self-maintenance and instinctive action architecture are mechanisms/dispositions rather than experiences, and major frameworks separately recognize motivation, arousal, sensation/perception, cognition, memory, mood, social processes, and sensorimotor control.
KILL 2 — "Science proves M -> I -> E -> F."
Rejected. Homeostatic/interoceptive feeling provides a plausible route to feeling that need not pass through a full emotion episode, and learned/contextual routes to emotion do not require one narrow innate policy as the sole mediator.
KILL 3 — "Instinct is a unitary genetically hardwired variable."
Rejected. Developmental and ethological literature shows that innateness-related properties dissociate. Species-typical behavior can depend on normal developmental experience.
KILL 4 — "Emotion is universally defined as a nonconscious functional state."
Rejected. This is one defensible scientific convention, strongly represented by Adolphs and colleagues, but other theories make subjective feeling central to emotion.
KILL 5 — "Feeling is directly measured by self-report."
Rejected. Report depends on access, metacognition, language, memory and demand characteristics; it is evidence about experience, not an identity definition.
KILL 6 — "Our mathematical equations are established laws."
Rejected. The equations in FOUR_CONSTRUCT_FORMAL_MODEL_v001 are candidate operational models chosen to make constructs testable.
KILL 7 — "We are the first to link homeostasis, affect, emotion, or feeling."
Rejected. Homeostasis/allostasis, interoception, active inference, homeostatic reinforcement learning, Damasio-style body-state feeling, Pankseppian affect, Human Affectome, and recent computational architectures all provide extensive prior art.
KILL 8 — "We are the first formal ontology separating emotion-related constructs."
Rejected. Emotion Ontology and related formal-ontology work already distinguishes dispositions, processes, experiences, action tendencies and representations.

2. PRIOR-ART PRESSURE
==================================================
HUMAN AFFECTOME
This is the strongest broad integrative prior art. It treats affect as a large conceptual space including sensation, feeling, emotion, mood, wellbeing and related constructs and organizes them in a nested/intertwined functional architecture. This sharply limits novelty claims based merely on placing viability beneath affect or separating feelings from other constructs.
RDoC
NIMH RDoC independently partitions negative and positive valence, cognitive, social, arousal/regulatory and sensorimotor domains. Arousal is explicitly distinguished from motivation and valence, and action planning/selection belongs to sensorimotor constructs. Thus a four-word exhaustive taxonomy is not credible.
EMOTION THEORY
The current literature still contains several major theory families rather than one accepted definition. A 2024 synthesis argues that major theories emphasize different emotion components: appraisal/elicitation, expression, feeling, action tendency, and bodily/autonomic activity. A new paper must therefore make its terminology conditional and explicit.
FORMAL/COMPUTATIONAL PRIOR ART
Body-regulation models already formalize homeostatic/allostatic control, interoception, action selection, reinforcement learning and active inference. Recent 2026 preprints also explicitly formalize motivated emotional architectures driven by homeostasis/allostatic violation. Therefore a broad "homeostasis causes emotion" model is not novel enough.

3. SURVIVING NOVELTY CANDIDATE
==================================================
The strongest contribution is the conjunction of five elements:
A. TYPED CONSTRUCT SEPARATION
Assign distinct formal roles:
M = viability-regulation process/objective;
I = developmentally canalized action disposition/policy prior;
E = occurrent latent multi-system coordination state;
F = phenomenal/experiential state with indirect measurement.
B. EXPLICIT REJECTION OF A SHARED SCALAR DEPTH AXIS
Show that the constructs belong to different mathematical types and therefore cannot be validly ranked merely by one "depth" variable.
C. EXHAUSTIVE FOUR-NODE ARCHITECTURE ENUMERATION
Enumerate the complete directed graph space for M,I,E,F:
4096 directed graphs without self-loops;
543 DAGs;
185 Markov-equivalence classes;
24 total linear orders.
This turns the verbal-order question into an identifiable causal-model question.
D. CONDITIONAL-INDEPENDENCE DESTRUCTION TEST
Derive the strict-chain constraints M independent E given I, M independent F given E, and I independent F given E under standard DAG assumptions, then show how a homeostatic/interoceptive bypass to feeling destroys the relevant independences.
E. PHENOMENAL IDENTIFIABILITY FIREWALL
Separate F_report from F_phen and prove/model that report prediction alone does not uniquely identify a phenomenal ontology. This prevents a formal construct paper from overclaiming consciousness.
Novelty status:
POSSIBLE, NOT YET VERIFIED AS UNIQUE.
A systematic novelty search must specifically look for prior papers combining all five elements above.
