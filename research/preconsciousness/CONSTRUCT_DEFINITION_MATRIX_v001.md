CONSTRUCT DEFINITION MATRIX v001
Date: 2026-10-07
Program: Pre-Consciousness Layer Program
Status: Stage-1 literature result; definitions remain operational hypotheses where the field lacks consensus.
RESEARCH QUESTION
Can self-maintenance, instinct, emotion, and feeling be given non-circular scientific definitions that are distinct enough to support a later mathematical and causal architecture test?
EXECUTIVE VERDICT
1. SELF-MAINTENANCE has the strongest scientific core of the four. Across physiology, homeostasis/allostasis research, and biological-autonomy work, the common idea is active maintenance or re-establishment of viability-compatible organization and physiological conditions despite internal and external perturbation.
2. INSTINCT does NOT currently behave like one clean scientific natural kind. Modern developmental and ethological literature shows that "innate," "hardwired," "genetically determined," "present at birth," and "independent of learning" are not equivalent. The project should therefore not use instinct as a primitive scalar. It should operationalize an instinct-like layer as a multidimensional construct describing species-typical, developmentally canalized action-control biases.
3. EMOTION also has no universally accepted definition. A strong operational intersection across affective-science approaches is a temporally bounded organism-level functional episode that coordinates multiple response systems when an event is evaluated as biologically or socially significant. This can include physiology, action tendency, attention/valuation, expression, learning, and decision effects. For project purposes, an "emotion-state" should be defined independently of subjective feeling so the emotion-versus-feeling boundary remains empirically testable.
4. FEELING has no single field-wide definition. At least two scientifically important meanings must be separated: (a) homeostatic/interoceptive feelings such as hunger, pain, thirst, and well-being; and (b) the subjective experience of an emotional episode. Reportability is a third construct, not identical to phenomenality.
CRITICAL CONSEQUENCE FOR THE FOUR-LAYER HYPOTHESIS
The current literature does NOT justify treating
self-maintenance -> instinct -> emotion -> feeling
as an established linear hierarchy.
The first link is plausible as a depth ordering: biological self-maintenance exists at levels of life that do not require complex nervous-system action programs.
The middle and final links are not yet established. "Instinct" decomposes into several developmental/action-control properties, and broad "feeling" may include homeostatic/interoceptive states that are not necessarily downstream of a full emotion episode.
Therefore the strict serial model remains a hypothesis, not a result.

LAYER 1 — SELF-MAINTENANCE
==================================================
FIELD STATUS
Relatively well grounded, though terminology differs across homeostasis, allostasis, autopoiesis, autonomy theory, and viability theory.
SCIENTIFIC CORE
A living system actively maintains, repairs, or re-establishes the organization and internal variables required for continued viability while exchanging matter and energy with its environment.
HOMEOSTASIS / ALLOSTASIS DISTINCTION
Homeostasis is classically centered on maintenance and defense of vital regulated variables.
Allostasis emphasizes adaptive or predictive change in regulatory activity so the organism remains viable under changing demands.
These are related regulatory strategies, not synonyms for "feeling," "instinct," or "emotion."
PROJECT OPERATIONAL DEFINITION M
Self-maintenance is the set of endogenous regulatory processes by which an organism preserves or recovers states inside a viability-compatible region despite perturbation.
STATE-SPACE FORM
Let x(t) be the organism state and V the viability region.
A candidate measure is:
M_H(x_t) = P[x(t:t+H) remains within V | current state and available regulation]
Alternative formal measures may use expected time-to-exit from V, viability kernels, or control cost required to remain in V.
MEASURABLE VARIABLES
- body temperature, glucose, osmolarity, oxygenation, pH, energy balance;
- error/deviation from regulated ranges;
- compensatory control signals;
- recovery after perturbation;
- repair and turnover;
- probability/time of remaining viable.
WHAT IT IS NOT
It is not by itself instinct, emotion, feeling, reasoning, or consciousness.
CANDIDATE FALSIFIER
If a proposed "self-maintenance" variable does not discriminate states or interventions that alter viability regulation from ordinary state changes, it is too broad to function as a scientific construct.

LAYER 2 — "INSTINCT" / CANALIZED ACTION-CONTROL
==================================================
FIELD STATUS
"Instinct" is scientifically overloaded and should not be treated as one primitive natural kind.
WHY THE OLD DEFINITION FAILS
The literature shows that the following properties can come apart:
- present at birth;
- species-typical;
- not individually learned;
- genetically influenced;
- developmentally canalized;
- stereotyped;
- evolutionarily selected;
- triggered by a specific cue;
- implemented by specialized neural circuitry.
Species-typical behaviors can depend on prenatal and postnatal experience while still developing reliably in normal ecological contexts.
PROJECT REPLACEMENT
Use the provisional construct:
CANALIZED ACTION-CONTROL ARCHITECTURE (CAA)
PROJECT OPERATIONAL DEFINITION I
A CAA is a developmentally reliable bias in perception-to-action or internal-state-to-action policy selection that emerges across a species-typical developmental range and does not require the organism to learn that policy through the same individual trial-and-error process needed for ordinary acquired habits.
IMPORTANT
This definition allows developmental input. It does not equate "instinctive" with genetically preprogrammed or experience-free.
FORMALIZATION
Do not use one scalar.
Represent I as a vector such as:
I = [C, D, L, S, T]
where
C = canalization/robustness across normal developmental environments;
D = developmental dependence;
L = dependence on individual learning;
S = species-typicality / population regularity;
T = trigger-policy specificity.
A policy representation can be:
P(pi | s, d, h)
where s = current stimulus/state,
d = developmental conditions,
h = individual learning history.
An instinct-like policy should remain strongly biased across normal d while showing lower dependence on h than a learned habit, but this must be measured rather than assumed.
MEASURABLE VARIABLES
- expression before task-specific training;
- reaction norm across developmental environments;
- policy reliability across individuals;
- change after sensory deprivation or altered developmental input;
- plasticity after learning;
- identified sensorimotor/motivational circuitry.
WHAT IT IS NOT
Not equivalent to reflex, fixed action pattern, drive, motivation, or "genetic program."
CANDIDATE FALSIFIER
If the proposed instinct-like behavior is fully explained by ordinary learning history or by immediate homeostatic reflex control, CAA adds no independent construct.

LAYER 3 — EMOTION-STATE
==================================================
FIELD STATUS
No universal scientific definition. Major theories disagree on whether emotions are basic biological states, constructed categories, appraisal processes, or conscious experiences.
USEFUL INTERSECTION
Several major approaches nevertheless converge on the idea that emotional episodes involve coordinated changes across multiple organismic systems in response to events that matter for needs, goals, threats, opportunities, or social relations.
PROJECT OPERATIONAL DEFINITION E
An emotion-state is a temporally bounded, event- or state-linked functional organization that coordinates multiple response systems — such as physiology, action readiness, attention/valuation, learning/decision biases, and expression — in relation to organism-relevant significance.
PROJECT CHOICE
Subjective feeling is NOT required in the operational definition of E.
This is deliberate, so the emotion-versus-feeling distinction can be tested rather than built into the definition.
FORMALIZATION
Let:
y_E(t) = [physiology, action tendency, attention/valuation, expression, learning/decision bias, ...]
and let e(t) be a latent emotion-state.
A candidate state-space model is:
e(t+1) = f_E(e(t), relevance/appraisal(t), body state(t), context(t))
y_E(t) = g_E(e(t)) + noise
The construct earns independent status only if the latent state or dynamical coupling predicts held-out multicomponent responses better than models containing only self-maintenance variables and canalized action policies.
MEASURABLE VARIABLES
- autonomic and endocrine measures;
- action readiness / approach-avoidance;
- attention allocation;
- reinforcement or learning modulation;
- expressive behavior;
- context-sensitive state transitions.
WHAT IT IS NOT
Not synonymous with facial expression, autonomic arousal, a single brain area, or a verbal label.
CANDIDATE FALSIFIER
If self-maintenance variables plus action-control policies fully predict the coordinated response pattern with no reproducible residual state structure, a distinct emotion-state layer is weakened.

LAYER 4 — FEELING
==================================================
FIELD STATUS
Definition not settled. The literature uses "feeling" in at least two major scientific senses.
TYPE A — HOMEOSTATIC / INTEROCEPTIVE FEELING
Examples proposed in the literature include hunger, thirst, pain, well-being, and other mental experiences of body state.
TYPE B — EMOTIONAL FEELING
The subjective experience associated with an emotional episode — e.g., what fear, anger, sadness, or joy feels like.
TYPE C — REPORT / ACCESS
A person's report or judgment about a felt state.
TYPE D — PHENOMENAL FEELING
The experience itself, considered separately from the ability to report it.
PROJECT REQUIREMENT
Do not collapse A, B, C, and D.
PROJECT OPERATIONAL VARIABLES
F_homeo(t) = reported or behaviorally indexed homeostatic/interoceptive feeling state.
F_emotion(t) = reported emotional experience linked to an emotion episode.
F_report(t) = explicit report/judgment/continuous rating.
F_phen(t) = phenomenal experience; currently no accepted third-person sufficient measure.
A statistical model may estimate:
P(F_report | E, interoceptive state, appraisal, access, context)
but this does NOT establish an identity between report and phenomenality.
CRITICAL RESULT
Broad "feeling" cannot safely be placed only after emotion, because homeostatic/interoceptive feelings are treated by influential theories as experiences of bodily regulation that need not be a full emotion episode.
CANDIDATE FALSIFIER FOR A SINGLE FEELING LAYER
If homeostatic feelings and emotional feelings show distinct causal/developmental architectures, the single Layer-4 construct must be split permanently.

ADJACENT-LAYER BOUNDARY STATUS
==================================================
SELF-MAINTENANCE vs INSTINCT-LIKE ACTION CONTROL
Current result: likely separable in principle.
Self-maintenance includes cellular/physiological regulation without requiring complex action policies.
Instinct-like action control concerns reliable perception/state-to-policy mappings.
Next empirical task: identify clean biological examples and formal dissociations.
INSTINCT-LIKE ACTION CONTROL vs EMOTION
Current result: unresolved.
Innate defensive, feeding, mating, and parental circuits can coordinate motivation, autonomic state, and action. This may make the boundary partly overlapping rather than strictly serial.
EMOTION vs FEELING
Current result: theory-dependent.
Scherer-style component models treat feeling as a subjective component of emotion.
Adolphs-style functional-state approaches distinguish emotion-state from its conscious experience.
LeDoux/Brown-style approaches emphasize conscious emotional experience while separating it from nonconscious survival circuits.
Damasio-style accounts include homeostatic feelings that broaden feeling beyond emotion.
Therefore there is no theory-neutral fact yet that "emotion comes first, feeling comes last."

REVISED ARCHITECTURE TO TEST
==================================================
Do not discard the original sequence, but stop treating it as the only candidate.
MODEL S — STRICT SERIAL
M -> I -> E -> F
MODEL B — BRANCHING
M -> viability/interoceptive regulation
M -> canalized action-control
canalized action-control + relevance/appraisal -> emotion-state
interoceptive regulation -> homeostatic feeling (in systems capable of feeling)
emotion-state -> emotional feeling (in systems capable of feeling)
MODEL R — RECURRENT
M <-> I <-> E
with feeling/report coupled to some states in conscious-capable systems.
The literature reviewed in Stage 1 currently makes B or R more defensible as competitors than assuming S is already correct.

SELF-MAINTENANCE vs CANALIZED ACTION-CONTROL.

Minimum evidence classes:
1. organisms/cells showing self-maintenance without nervous-system action programs;
