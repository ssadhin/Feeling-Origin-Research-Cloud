==================================================
N27 — CONSCIOUS DESIRE / URGE
==================================================
WORKING DEFINITION
A consciously experienced motivational state directed toward an action, object or outcome, typically involving felt pull/pressure/readiness to obtain, avoid, consume or act.
==================================================
1. N27 vs N25 MOTIVATIONAL CONTROL STATE
==================================================
N25 can be unconscious.
Motivational systems can alter vigor, direction and persistence without a corresponding conscious report.
Berridge-style incentive salience can be activated without conscious desire and can dissociate from explicit cognitive wanting.
VERDICT
N27 is not identical to motivation.
N25 is a functional motivational state.
N27 is a phenomenal/cognitive-motivational experience.
RELATION
N25 can contribute to N27.
N27 can influence deliberate action but is not required for all motivated action.
==================================================
2. N27 vs INCENTIVE SALIENCE "WANTING"
==================================================
Incentive salience is a motivational process that can be cue-triggered and unconscious.
Ordinary cognitive wanting/desire is goal-representational and can be consciously experienced.
VERDICT
DISTINCT BUT INTERACTING.
Do not use "wanting" without quotation/definition because the literature uses it for different constructs.
NO NEW TARGET DEFINITION GENERATED.
==================================================
3. N27 vs HEDONIC "LIKING"
==================================================
Reward literature demonstrates dissociation between "wanting" and "liking."
VERDICT
N27 desire/urge is not pleasure.
Pleasure belongs to an affective/feeling subtype; desire belongs to motivational/goal-directed phenomenology.
CONSEQUENCE
F cannot be reduced to positive/negative valence or pleasure.
==================================================
4. N27 vs ACTION TENDENCY / URGENCY
==================================================
Action tendency:
functional readiness/disposition toward action.
Urgency signal:
can influence decision commitment and action vigor without necessarily defining a conscious urge.
Conscious urge:
phenomenal motivational experience.
VERDICT
Separate:
functional action pressure;
neural/decision urgency;
phenomenal urge.
This is another type distinction, not a new fundamental target.
==================================================
5. N27 vs EMOTIONAL FEELING
==================================================
Many emotional feelings contain urges/action tendencies, but desire can occur outside a discrete emotion episode.
Examples:
ordinary wanting for food;
urge to move;
urge to breathe;
goal-directed desire.
VERDICT
N27 is not reducible to F_e.
Best classification:
N27 belongs to the broader phenomenal feeling family as a motivational/urge subtype, while its upstream generators belong to motivation/action-control systems.
UPDATE TO F TAXONOMY
Add:
F_u = motivational/urge feeling.
This is a subtype addition, not a new top-level definition of F.
==================================================
6. N27 vs METACOGNITIVE FEELING
==================================================
Metacognitive feelings concern one's own cognitive processing or epistemic state.
Urges/desires concern motivational/action targets.
VERDICT
DISTINCT SUBFAMILIES inside broad phenomenal/felt experience.
==================================================

==================================================
