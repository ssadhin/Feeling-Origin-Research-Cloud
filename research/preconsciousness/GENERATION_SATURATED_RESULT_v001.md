1. M* — BIOLOGICAL SELF-MAINTENANCE
==================================================
DEFINITION
Biological self-maintenance is the coupled endogenous organization by which a living system:
(1) regulates physiological/functional trajectories so that they remain within or return to recoverable viability bounds; and
(2) repairs, regenerates or reproduces the organization/constraints that make continued endogenous regulation possible.
TWO ANALYTIC TIERS
M_const:
constitutive maintenance — regeneration/repair/turnover of enabling organization.
M_reg:
regulatory maintenance — reactive or predictive control of viability-relevant variables.
M_pred:
optional analytic subtype for anticipatory/allostatic control.
These tiers may be deeply coupled biologically; the split is analytic, not a claim of separate anatomical modules.
TYPE
Constitutive + regulatory process/constraint system.
FORMALIZATION
Let:
x_t = physiological/functional state;
c_t = constitutive organizational/constraint state;
u_t = endogenous regulatory/repair action;
w_t = disturbance.
Dynamics:
x_(t+1) = f_x(x_t,c_t,u_t,w_t)
c_(t+1) = f_c(c_t,x_t,u_t,w_t)
Define admissible living region:
A = {(x,c) : viability constraints on x hold and enabling organization c remains within a regenerable range}.
Horizon self-maintenance functional:
M*_H(x_t,c_t,pi) =
P_pi[(x_(t+k),c_(t+k)) in A for all k=0,...,H | x_t,c_t].
Alternative measures:
expected first-exit time from A;
viability kernel;
minimum recovery/control cost;
repair/replacement rates;
prediction/regulation error;
resilience after perturbation.
DISTINCTION
M* is about continued viable organization and its regulation.
It is not:
a prepared behavioral bias;
an emotion episode;
a phenomenal feeling;
or mere survival probability.
SURVIVAL PROBABILITY WARNING

Therefore survival/persistence is an outcome metric, not the definition.

2. I* — DEVELOPMENTALLY CANALIZED PREPAREDNESS PROFILE
==================================================
WORD VERDICT
Broad ordinary "instinct" does not survive as one scientific natural kind.
Genetically determined, inborn, present at birth, experience-independent, species-typical, stereotyped, prepared, and unlearned are not equivalent properties.

I* = Developmentally Canalized Preparedness Profile (DCPP).
DEFINITION
I* is a multidimensional profile of developmentally reliable priors/biases in perception/attention, learning/association, valuation/motivation, and action/policy selection that:
(a) recur with measurable robustness across a specified normal developmental/ecological range;
(b) are not fully explained by the individual's task-specific acquired history;
(c) may nevertheless require ordinary developmental experience;
(d) may have different mechanisms in different domains.
TYPE
Family/profile of dispositions/priors, not one scalar state.
DOMAIN PROFILE

[B_P, B_L, B_V, B_A, C_D, R_pop, H_task]

It is not:

EVOLUTION WARNING
