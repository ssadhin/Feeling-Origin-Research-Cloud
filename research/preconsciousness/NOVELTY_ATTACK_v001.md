Target conjunction:
self-maintenance / homeostasis

VERDICT
No source found in this targeted pass that exactly combines all six elements into the same framework and performs an exhaustive four-node architecture enumeration. However, every individual component has substantial prior art, and several papers overlap with two or more components. Therefore novelty can only be claimed for the specific formal synthesis and derived causal tests, not for the underlying concepts.
STRONGEST PRIOR-ART ATTACKS
1. HUMAN AFFECTOME
Broad integrative map of affective science including feeling, sensation, emotion, mood, wellbeing, motivation, stress, self and related constructs, organized by nested/intertwined functional purposes. It is major prior art against any claim that M/I/E/F is an exhaustive affect taxonomy or that viability-based organization is novel.
2. EMOTION ONTOLOGY / MFOEM
Hastings, Ceusters, Smith & Mulligan already formalized distinctions between emotion occurrents/processes, emotion dispositions, traits, emotional action tendencies, physiological responses and subjective feelings under Basic Formal Ontology.
Consequence: typed distinctions between dispositions, processes and feelings are not novel by themselves.
3. COMPONENTIAL EMOTION THEORIES / 2024 AFFECTIVISM SYNTHESIS
Dukes & Sander (2024) explicitly characterize emotion as interacting components and argue that five major emotion-theory families emphasize different components: appraisal/elicitation, expression, feeling, action tendency and autonomic activity.
Consequence: treating emotion as a multi-component process is not novel.
4. ADOLPHS FUNCTIONAL-STATE PROGRAM
Adolphs (2017) and Adolphs & Andler (2018) explicitly distinguish functional emotion states from conscious emotion experiences/feelings and from concepts and expressions.
Consequence: E != F under a functional convention is not novel.
5. DAMASIO & CARVALHO
Feelings as mental experiences of body states, including hunger, pain, wellbeing, fear, anger and social feelings.
Consequence: homeostatic/interoceptive routes to feeling and distinctions between regulatory body state and feeling are established prior art.
6. DENTON ET AL. "PRIMORDIAL EMOTIONS"
A direct conceptual rival: calls thirst, hunger for air/food, pain and mineral hunger "primordial emotions" and describes them as the subjective element of instincts serving homeostasis.
Consequence: terminology across homeostasis, instinct, emotion and feeling is theory-dependent. A new framework must explicitly show that its definitions are operational conventions rather than the uniquely correct meanings of ordinary words.
7. HOMEOSTATIC / COMPUTATIONAL CONTROL MODELS
Petzschner et al.; Keramati & Gutkin; active-inference and homeostatic RL work formally model body regulation, drive, action selection and learning.
Consequence: mathematical regulation/action coupling is not novel.
8. LOWE & ZIEMKE 2011
Reviews bidirectional relations among homeostasis, action tendencies and emotional feelings and proposes feeling as prediction of action tendency.
Consequence: feedback/recurrent emotion-feeling-action loops are not novel.
9. RECENT 2026 MATHEMATICAL PRIOR ART
Galus & Starzyk (arXiv 2026) present a motivated emotional architecture integrating homeostasis/allostasis, motivation, affect, memory and action selection.
Consequence: broad "formal homeostasis -> motivation/affect/action" novelty is already under heavy pressure.
10. HOMEOSTASIS AND CAUSAL CONTROL (WEINBERGER 2026)
Links mathematical homeostatic control to dynamic causal models and interventionist causal analysis.
Consequence: causal formalization of homeostasis itself is not novel.
11. CAUSAL EMOTION NETWORK MAPPING
Prior work combines causal discovery and stimulation/fMRI to investigate emotion networks.
Consequence: using causal graphs in emotion research is not novel.
SURVIVING NOVELTY WINDOW
Potentially novel conjunction:
- use one explicit cross-level typed ontology spanning viability regulation (M), canalized action disposition (I), functional emotion-state (E), and phenomenal feeling (F);
- formally demonstrate that the original verbal chain is underidentified by exhaustive enumeration of the complete four-node graph space;
- derive the strict chain's conditional-independence constraints;
- use homeostatic/interoceptive feeling as an explicit counterexample/bypass that falsifies the universal mediation claim;
- include a phenomenal-report measurement model that blocks inference from report prediction to phenomenal identity;
- convert these into preregisterable empirical tests.
This is a SYNTHESIS/METHODS novelty, not a discovery of the underlying constructs.
NOVELTY KILL CONDITION
If a prior paper is found that already combines these typed four constructs, exhaustive causal-architecture enumeration, the same conditional-independence analysis, and the phenomenal-identifiability firewall, then the current paper's core novelty is killed or must be reduced to implementation/application.
