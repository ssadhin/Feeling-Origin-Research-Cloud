"Emotion is simply homeostatic/allostatic regulation."

PAIR 4 — I vs E
==================================================
COLLAPSE OPTION
"Emotion is an instinctive action program."
ATTACK
Basic-emotion and fear-module theories give this collapse some local plausibility because evolved response systems couple elicitation, action and physiology.
However:
- action tendencies are only one component in componential theories;
- constructionist accounts do not require fixed inherited emotion programs;
- learned/contextual appraisals can elicit emotion;
- action-selection circuitry is modulated by drive, arousal and emotion rather than identical to those internal states.
VERDICT
UNIVERSAL COLLAPSE FAILS.
Prepared affective modules may exist as specific composite mechanisms, but they are not a definition of all emotion.
RELATION
I can BIAS E elicitation/expression.
E can MODULATE action selection within I/action systems.
NEW COMPOSITE?
