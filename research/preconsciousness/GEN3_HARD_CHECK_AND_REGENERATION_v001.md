M11 — TWO-TIER SELF-MAINTENANCE
==================================================
CLAIM
Self-maintenance contains at least:
Tier C — constitutive maintenance/regeneration of enabling organization/constraints.
Tier R — regulatory maintenance of viability-relevant variables by reactive or predictive control.
ATTACK 1 — ARE C AND R ALWAYS SEPARABLE?
No. Biological autonomy literature often treats regulation as operating on/within constitutively maintained organization. A clean module boundary is not guaranteed.
ATTACK 2 — IS C REQUIRED FOR EVERY EMPIRICAL USE OF "SELF-MAINTENANCE"?
No. Physiological homeostasis studies can validly isolate R without measuring organizational closure.
ATTACK 3 — IS R REQUIRED FOR EVERY CONSTITUTIVE SELF-MAINTAINING SYSTEM?
Some minimal/autopoietic models emphasize self-production without rich sensor-effector regulation. Therefore C does not reduce to R.
ATTACK 4 — DOES THIS GENERATE A NEW TYPE BEYOND M10?
No. M11 is a useful decomposition of M10 rather than a materially different rival.
STATUS
PASS AS DECOMPOSITION; EQUIVALENT-IN-SCOPE to M10 when M10 is explicitly two-component.
No new M candidate generated.

I17 — PREPAREDNESS FAMILY
==================================================
CLAIM
"Instinct-like" organization may comprise distinct prepared architectures rather than one policy mechanism:
reflex preparedness;
motor-pattern preparedness;
policy-selection preparedness;
possibly perceptual/attentional preparedness.
ATTACK 1 — IS ACTION POLICY TOO NARROW?
Yes, potentially.
Developmental evidence shows perception, action and representation can be functionally dissociable and co-develop through intrinsic constraints plus experience. Preparedness research also concerns selective learning/attention, not only motor output.
ATTACK 2 — DOES "PREPAREDNESS" ITSELF HAVE A SINGLE ESTABLISHED MECHANISM?
No. Fear-preparedness literature contains competing explanations and systematic-review evidence weakens some classic extinction predictions.
ATTACK 3 — CAN A PURE ACTION-DISPOSITION MODEL CAPTURE SELECTIVE LEARNING BIASES?
Not necessarily. A system may be biologically/developmentally biased to learn certain associations faster without possessing a fixed pre-task action policy.
RESULT
I17 reveals a real residual:
Generation-0 ICAD and Generation-2 I16 were still action-centric.
NEW CANDIDATE
I18 — DEVELOPMENTALLY CANALIZED PREPAREDNESS PROFILE.
I18 definition:
a multidimensional set of developmentally reliable priors/biases in perception, learning, valuation, motivation and action that emerge across a specified normal ecological/developmental range and are not reducible to the individual's task-specific acquired history.
I18 does NOT assert:
genetic preprogramming;
experience-independence;
one neural module;
one scalar "instinct" quantity.
STATUS
I17 PARTIAL.
I18 generated as materially broader candidate.

E17 — EMOTION-ORGANIZATION FAMILY
==================================================
CLAIM
There may be multiple mechanistically distinct emotion organizations sharing higher-level functional criteria rather than one universal emotion-state mechanism.
ATTACK 1 — DOES THIS SIMPLY SAY "EMOTION IS WHATEVER WE CALL EMOTION"?
Risk exists.
A family requires explicit membership criteria.
PROPOSED MEMBERSHIP CRITERIA
An E-family member must be an organism-relevant episode/trajectory showing at least:
(a) temporally structured change rather than a static label;
(b) coupling among multiple components;
(c) action/valuation significance;
(d) context-sensitive persistence, scaling or generalization;
(e) measurable causal consequences beyond one isolated output.
ATTACK 2 — DO ALL THEORIES REQUIRE ALL THESE FEATURES?
No. Constructionist and basic-emotion accounts may disagree over category structure and mechanisms.
However, the componential synthesis shows broad convergence on multiple interacting components even while theories privilege different ones.
ATTACK 3 — DOES E17 GENERATE A NEW OPTION BEYOND E16?
Not materially.
E16 already permits distributed organization, optional latent compression and theory-dependent full episodes.
E17 is best represented as the family-level interpretation of E16.
STATUS
PASS AS FAMILY INTERPRETATION; NO NEW E CANDIDATE.

CLAIM

I18 — NEW GENERATION-3 CANDIDATE
==================================================
DEFINITION
A Developmentally Canalized Preparedness Profile (DCPP) is a multidimensional set of developmentally reliable biases/priors across:
P = perception/attention;
L = learning/association;
V = valuation/motivation;
A = action/policy selection,
that recur across a defined species-typical developmental range and cannot be explained solely by the individual's task-specific learning history.
FORMALIZATION
Let domain q belong to {P,L,V,A}.
For each domain:
B_q(s,d) = pre-task or developmentally prior bias function.
Preparedness profile:
I18 = [B_P, B_L, B_V, B_A, C_dev, H_task_dependence, population regularity].
A learning-preparedness term can be represented as differential sample efficiency:
G_L = E[N_ref - N_prepared]
under matched tasks, with confounds controlled.
A perceptual-preparedness term can be represented as prior/likelihood bias:
P(percept | input,d) proportional to P(input|percept) P_0(percept|d).
An action-preparedness term remains:
P_0(pi|s,d).
CRITICAL RULE
These are examples of domain-specific operationalizations; no single common numerical scale is assumed.
ATTACKS
A. Could all apparent preparedness arise from early unmeasured experience?
Yes in some cases. Therefore developmental manipulations/reaction norms are required.
B. Could evolutionary relevance be inferred merely because a bias exists?
No. Selection history requires independent phylogenetic/comparative evidence.
C. Does broad DCPP become too inclusive?
Potentially. Membership requires robust pre-task bias plus defined developmental reproducibility and contrast against ordinary acquired learning.
D. Does DCPP still deserve the word "instinct"?
Only as an explicit project translation. Scientific reporting should use the narrower DCPP components.
STATUS
PASS AS THE BROADEST CURRENT OPERATIONAL REPLACEMENT FOR BROAD "INSTINCT."
I16 is retained as the ACTION subprofile of I18.
