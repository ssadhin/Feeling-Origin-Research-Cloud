3. EMOTION
==================================================
GENERATION 0
Emotion E =
occurrent temporally extended latent internal state coordinating multiple systems.
Formula:
e_(t+1)=f_E(e_t,...)+eta
y_t^(k)=g_k(e_t)+noise.
Feeling deliberately excluded.
SATURATED
Emotion E* =
an explicit two-level operational framework:
E_func =
temporally structured organism-relevant coordination trajectory across appraisal/relevance, physiology, action readiness, attention/valuation, expression, learning/decision systems.
E_episode =
full theory-dependent emotion episode; some theories may include conscious feeling.
Formula:
z_t=[component vector]
z_(t+1)=F_E(z_t,body,context,history)+epsilon.
A latent e_t is OPTIONAL model compression, not required ontology.
WHAT STAYED THE SAME
- occurrent rather than disposition;
- temporal persistence/structure;
- organism relevance;
- multisystem coordination;
- distinct from one motor program;
- operational E/F distinction remains possible.
WHAT CHANGED
This is a MAJOR ONTOLOGICAL REFINEMENT.
Generation 0 implicitly treated a central latent emotion-state as the preferred form.
Recursive theory comparison showed that this overcommits.
A distributed coordination trajectory is more theory-neutral.
Also:
feeling cannot simply be excluded from every meaning of "emotion" because some serious theories include it as a component of the full episode.
Therefore:
E_func excludes feeling by operational choice;
E_episode remains theory-dependent.
The 2025 basic-emotion versus constructed-emotion debate further blocks any claim that this split reconciles the field.
SIMILARITY VERDICT
MODERATE-HIGH in functional core;
MAJOR CHANGE in ontology/terminology.
CHANGE TYPE
RETYPE / DE-CENTRALIZATION / DUAL-LEVEL DISTINCTION.
DID GEN0 SURVIVE?
YES AS AN OPTIONAL LATENT MODEL OF E_func, NOT AS THE DEFINITIVE EMOTION ONTOLOGY.
