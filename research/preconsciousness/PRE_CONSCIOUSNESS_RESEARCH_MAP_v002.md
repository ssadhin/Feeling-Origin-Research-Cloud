==================================================

Self-maintenance -> Instinct -> Emotion -> Feeling
Status:
FAILED-RIVAL as a universal strict causal chain.

Main breaks:
- broad instinct is not one natural kind;
- feeling contains non-emotional subfamilies;
- learned/contextual routes to emotion bypass a mandatory narrow instinct stage;
- mature architecture is recurrent;
- four categories are not exhaustive.
