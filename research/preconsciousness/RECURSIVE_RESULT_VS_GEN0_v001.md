EXECUTIVE COMPARISON
==================================================
HIGH-LEVEL SIMILARITY
Generation 0 and the recursive result agree on the central negative findings:
- the strict Self-maintenance -> Instinct -> Emotion -> Feeling ladder is not a universal law;
- broad "instinct" is not one clean natural kind;
- feeling is heterogeneous and must be separated from report;
- the targets occupy different scientific/ontological roles;
- motivation, arousal, interoception, cognition/learning and other neighboring variables cannot be silently collapsed into the four.
MAJOR DIFFERENCE
The recursive method shows that Generation 0 was still too compressed.
Generation 0:
M = one regulatory process.
I = one canalized action disposition.
E = one latent functional state.
F = one phenomenal state/family.
Recursive result:
M decomposes into constitutive self-maintenance, regulatory self-maintenance, and a separate viability criterion.
I decomposes into prepared reflex pathways, prepared/canalized policy dispositions, and prepared motor-pattern generators; drive/motivation stays separate.
E is best defined theory-neutrally as a coordinated dynamical episode/trajectory; a central latent state is only one implementation hypothesis.
F is best represented as a content-indexed phenomenal family, not one terminal causal node; a shared phenomenality mechanism remains an open consciousness hypothesis.
The full system must distinguish ontology, mechanism, and measurement/access.
Thus Generation 0 is best understood as a LOWER-RESOLUTION PROJECTION of the recursive result, not as a wholly different theory.

1. SELF-MAINTENANCE COMPARISON
==================================================
GENERATION 0
Definition:
Active endogenous regulation preserving/restoring physiological or organizational variables within a viability-compatible region.
Formula:
x_(t+1) = f_M(x_t,u_t,w_t)
V = {x : g_j(x) <= 0}
M_H(x_t,pi_M) =
P[x_(t+k) in V for all k=0,...,H | x_t]
Strength:
Already mechanism-oriented and viability-based.
Problem exposed by recursion:
It bundles three different things:
1. the criterion of viability;
2. constitutive self-production/repair;
3. regulatory control that counters perturbation.
RECURSIVE RESULT
Let:
x_t = fast physiological/regulatory state.
o_t = constitutive/organizational state.
u_t^R = regulatory control.
u_t^C = constitutive/repair activity.
Candidate coupled dynamics:
x_(t+1) = f_R(x_t,o_t,u_t^R,w_t^R)
o_(t+1) = f_C(o_t,x_t,u_t^C,w_t^C)
Define viability region over the joint organism state:
V subset of X × O.
Horizon viability:
V_H(x_t,o_t) =
P[(x_(t+k),o_(t+k)) in V for all k=0,...,H | current state and available controls]
Interpretation:
M_R = regulatory maintenance.
M_C = constitutive maintenance/self-production.
V = viability criterion/constraint.
VERDICT
SIMILAR CORE, IMPORTANT DECOMPOSITION.
Generation 0 survives but must be split.

GENERATION 0
Operational replacement:
ICAD = developmentally canalized action disposition.
Core formalization:
P_0(pi | s,d)
C_I(s) =

"Instinct" remains an umbrella/historical term.
Mechanistic decomposition:
I_R — PREPARED REFLEX
P(a_t | s_t, h_t)

I_P — PREPARED/CANALIZED POLICY DISPOSITION
P_0(pi | s,d)

g_(t+1) = f_G(g_t,s_t,modulators_t)

d_(t+1) = f_D(d_t,body_need_t,incentive_t,context_t)

P(pi_t | s_t,d_t,I_P,h_t)

3. EMOTION COMPARISON
==================================================
GENERATION 0
Definition:
Functional emotion-state = temporally extended latent internal state coordinating multiple systems.
Formula:
e_(t+1) = f_E(e_t,z_t,x_t,c_t,theta_E) + eta_t
y_t^(k) = g_k(e_t) + xi_t^(k)
Strength:
Makes persistence, scalability, generalization and multisystem coordination testable.
Separates function from conscious feeling under an explicit convention.
Problem exposed by recursion:
"Latent central state" is an implementation assumption, not required by the common evidence.
A distributed network/component process can generate the same observable coordination.
RECURSIVE RESULT
Theory-neutral definition:
EMOTION COORDINATION EPISODE (ECE) =
a temporally bounded trajectory of coordinated change across specified components in relation to organism-relevant conditions.
Component vector:
q_t =
[appraisal/relevance,
 valence/core affect,
 arousal,
 autonomic/endocrine state,
 action tendency/motivation,
 attention,
 expression,
 learning/decision bias,
 interoceptive representation,
 context-dependent components, ...]
Dynamics:
q_(t+1) =
F_E(q_t,s_t,body_t,history_t,context_t) + eta_t
Episode class:
E_j =
{q_(t:t+T) : trajectory belongs to validated coordinated class C_j}
Two implementation rivals remain:
E_component — no extra causal E entity; emotion is the coordinated component trajectory.
E_latent — a latent e_t causally coordinates components.
E_latent earns independent status only if it gives reproducible held-out and causal/interventional information beyond the component dynamics themselves.
VERDICT
SAME CORE IDEA OF MULTISYSTEM COORDINATION, BUT THE RECURSIVE RESULT REMOVES AN UNNECESSARY CENTRAL-LATENT-STATE COMMITMENT.
This is one of the largest changes from Generation 0.

4. FEELING COMPARISON
==================================================
GENERATION 0
Definition:
Feeling = first-person qualitative/phenomenal state.
Subtypes:
homeostatic/interoceptive;
emotional;
possibly sensory-affective;
report vs phenomenality separated.
Measurement:
r_t ~ P(r | phi_t,access_t,language_t,demand_t,memory_t)
Strength:
Correctly separates phenomenality from report and already recognizes more than one feeling subtype.
Problems exposed by recursion:
1. "first-person phenomenal state" is too broad if it makes every visual/auditory/thought experience a feeling.
2. One phi_t suggests more unity than current evidence supports.
3. Metacognitive/epistemic, agentive/ownership, pain dimensions and other feeling families have distinct proximal generators.
4. A single terminal F node falsely suggests one upstream pathway.
RECURSIVE RESULT
Feeling is represented as a CONTENT-INDEXED PHENOMENAL FAMILY:
F_t^(c)
where content-family c can include:
homeostatic/interoceptive;
sensory-affective;
emotional;
mood/background;
metacognitive/epistemic;
agentive/ownership;
social/relational;
urge/action-related;
other validated families.
Measurement:
r_t^(c) ~
P(r | F_t^(c),Access_t,language_t,memory_t,demand_t)
Optional common-phenomenality hypothesis:
F_t^(c) = Phi(z_t^(c),kappa_t)
where Phi/kappa are placeholders for a common phenomenal-enabling mechanism.
IMPORTANT
The existence of one common Phi is NOT established.
If different feeling families require different phenomenal mechanisms, even this common operator must be abandoned.
Pain refinement:
F_pain may itself require at least:
sensory-discriminative quality;
affective unpleasantness/motivation;
secondary significance.
VERDICT
GENERATION 0'S PHENOMENAL/REPORT FIREWALL SURVIVES.
THE ONE-NODE/ONE-PHI-TYPE REPRESENTATION DOES NOT.
Feeling is better treated as a family indexed by content/generator, with common phenomenality left as an open hypothesis.

If M, I, E and F are each treated as one node, there are 4,096 directed graphs without self-loops.

The enumeration remains scientifically useful for testing the original four-word hypothesis but is not exhaustive of the refined mechanistic space.

9. CURRENT BEST RESULT AFTER RECURSION
==================================================
The four original words should be treated as FOUR RESEARCH HEADINGS, not four atomic natural kinds:
SELF-MAINTENANCE
= umbrella over constitutive self-maintenance M_C and regulatory self-maintenance M_R, evaluated relative to viability constraints V.
INSTINCT
= historical umbrella over prepared action architecture; mechanistically split at least into prepared reflex I_R, prepared policy disposition I_P, and prepared pattern generator I_G, with drive/motivation D separate.
EMOTION
= emotion coordination episode ECE, a temporally organized multisystem trajectory; whether a separate latent emotion state exists is an empirical model-selection question.
FEELING
= content-indexed phenomenal family F^(c); homeostatic, emotional, metacognitive, agentive, sensory-affective and other families may have different proximal generators. Report/access is not feeling itself.
