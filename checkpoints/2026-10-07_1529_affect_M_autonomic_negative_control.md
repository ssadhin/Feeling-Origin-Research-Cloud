Therefore autonomic response is not sufficient evidence for affective feeling. A newer 2026 double-blind TUS study of S1/VPL changes heat-pain/warm-detection thresholds, demonstrating sensory modulation but not affect-specific unpleasantness; it is a useful sensory rival/control, not evidence for M.
