BUILD
Rainville et al. 1997 (Science; PMID 9252330) manipulated hypnotic suggestions to selectively alter pain unpleasantness while perceived intensity was held approximately unchanged; PET changes tracked unpleasantness in ACC while primary somatosensory activation was unchanged. Hofbauer et al. 2001 (J Neurophysiol; DOI 10.1152/jn.2001.86.1.402) used the complementary manipulation aimed at pain intensity and reported S1 modulation, contrasting with the earlier unpleasantness/ACC result. Together the authors characterize this as a double dissociation of sensory-discriminative versus affective pain processing.

ATTACK
This is NOT the proof target's required double causal dissociation. The intervention is cognitive suggestion/hypnosis, not selective perturbation of ACC or a formal mechanism M. ACC activity is measured rather than intervened upon. The intensity-directed experiment also changed unpleasantness secondarily, and intensity/unpleasantness were highly correlated. Neither study demonstrates preserved independent reasoning/access under the affect manipulation. Therefore the pair supports separability of pain affect from sensory intensity but cannot establish ACC as necessary/sufficient for affective feeling or phenomenality.

RE-ATTACK OF REGIONAL M
Human intracranial electrical stimulation evidence (Guillory & Bujarski 2019; PMID 30843590) shows stimulation of ACC/OFC/insula can evoke negatively valenced subjective experiences, with stimulation magnitude related to reported experience intensity. But stimulation also evokes somatic/visceral/olfactory/gustatory sensations depending on site, so it fails clean affect-specificity and cannot isolate a single regional M. Existing lesion counterexamples preserved in prior checkpoints further defeat simple ACC/insula necessity.

REFINEMENT
Current candidate M must be distributed/functional rather than 'ACC activity'. A defensible formal target is an affect-specific transformation A that maps sensory evidence + organism state/history/context into affective significance while being statistically/causally separable from sensory coding, autonomic response, report, access, metacognition and reasoning. This is only a target class, not a discovered mechanism.

FROZEN PREDICTION / KILL CONDITIONS
Before any new outcome analysis: an intervention on candidate A must shift unpleasantness/valence beyond prespecified equivalence margins while sensory discrimination/intensity and reasoning remain approximately preserved; a converse reasoning/access perturbation must impair reasoning/access while affect remains approximately preserved. Require held-out prediction and rival comparison against sensory intensity, generic salience/arousal, autonomic state, demand/report, and valuation-only models. Kill A if affect specificity disappears after these controls, if a simpler rival matches it, if independent replication fails, or if reasoning/access impairment necessarily abolishes affect.

NOVELTY / PUBLICATION AUDIT
Rainville/Hofbauer sensory-affective dissociation is classic prior art, not novel. The 2019 iES result is causal evidence for affective-state generation but not affect-specific enough for M. No genuinely paper-worthy proof-grade result emerged. The potentially publishable contribution remains the stricter cross-dataset causal-dissociation framework if it yields a preregistered discriminating result.
