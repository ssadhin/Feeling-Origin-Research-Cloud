This is a modality-independent replication target for a signal first nominated in ds005289 EEG.

Frozen prediction before outcome testing: derive M on ds005289 to predict unpleasantness beyond prespecified stimulus energy, intensity and nuisance variables. No feature selection or threshold may use ds005883 outcomes. Test only preregistered coarse hypotheses transferable across EEG/fMRI. On ds005883 require incremental held-out prediction of unpleasantness beyond temperature/intensity and generic salience/autonomic rivals. Positive result nominates affective representation, not phenomenal presence P.

Kill conditions: no held-out increment in ds005289; disappearance after intensity/energy/report controls; failure in ds005883; generic salience/autonomic rival matches or beats M; signal tracks sensory intensity equally; or causal interventions fail affect-selective change with sensory discrimination/reasoning approximately preserved.
