## Attack and prior art
Published Mąka et al. result (PMID 41344638) already reports active tDCS changes in self-reported social affect with ERP dissociation and reappraisal findings. Therefore published direction is NOT novel, and any future analysis of this dataset is outcome-exposed, not genuinely blind discovery. Stimulus content, appraisal/reappraisal instruction, demand, blinding, physiological arousal, rating gain and EEG salience are serious rivals. No objective reasoning decrement and no sensory intensity/discrimination equivalence => not a reverse-arm double dissociation. No report-to-phenomenality logical proof.
Independent randomized pain forward-arm negative comparator: Li et al. 2024 DOI 10.1038/s42003-024-07129-x, N=115 analyzed (M1 39, DLPFC 39, sham 37), rated both intensity and unpleasantness after calibrated laser stimuli. Published delayed group intensity F(2,112)=4.14 p=.018, unpleasantness F(2,112)=1.89 p=.16; immediate unpleasantness F(2,112)=.18 p=.83. Difference between a significant intensity p and nonsignificant unpleasantness p is NOT a tested intensity-vs-unpleasantness interaction and nonsignificance is NOT equivalence. No affect-specific intervention demonstrated; no Q/A converse, no unique M. Source https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-07129-x .
