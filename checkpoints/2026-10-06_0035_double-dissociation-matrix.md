## Frozen prediction before evidence extraction
Candidate M must support a selective causal affect signature: perturbing M changes unpleasantness/valence more than sensory intensity/discrimination, while basic reasoning remains approximately preserved. Converse arm: perturbing reasoning/access should impair reasoning/access more than affective experience. No behavioral/report result alone establishes P; bridge assumption remains explicit.

## Evidence attack
Rainville et al. Science 1997 (PMID 9252330): hypnotic suggestion selectively altered pain unpleasantness without perceived intensity change; ACC activity tracked unpleasantness while S1 did not. Strong dissociation but intervention is cognitive and imaging correlational for ACC, so it does not establish ACC as M.
Zeidan et al. 2011/2012 mindfulness (PMID 22172578): practitioners reduced unpleasantness ~22% and anticipatory anxiety ~29%, associated with decreased lateral PFC and increased posterior-insula processing. This directly attacks a simple "more cognitive control => less affect" account but is not a focal causal perturbation and does not prove phenomenality.
Goel et al. 2017 (PMID 28263798): focal polar/orbital PFC lesions selectively impaired reasoning with emotional material. Useful reasoning/affect interaction evidence, but it does NOT establish preserved affective experience under impaired reasoning; therefore converse dissociation remains unsatisfied.
Zhang et al. 2024 (PMID 38838979): online TMS over VLPFC/DLPFC altered negative feeling ratings. This shows frontal cognitive-control/semantic nodes can causally modulate affect, undermining any clean anatomical separation between reasoning circuitry and feeling generation.
